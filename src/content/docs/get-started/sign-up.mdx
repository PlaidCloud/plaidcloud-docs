Signing up for PlaidCloud is one short flow that ends with you working inside your own workspace. You verify who you are, name the workspace, pick a plan, and — while the workspace builds itself — invite your team. When it's ready you choose a password once and land on your workspace Home with a guided first run waiting for you.

## Verify Your Account

Start from a plan on the [pricing page](https://plaidcloud.com/), or open the [PlaidCloud app](https://app.plaidcloud.com) directly.

<Steps>

1. **Confirm your email.** Continue with Google, GitHub, or Microsoft, or enter your email address and type the six-digit code we send you. Either way, this only confirms your address — you'll choose a PlaidCloud password later, once.

2. **Say what you want to do first.** One optional question tailors the sample project and connector suggestions waiting for you inside the workspace. You can skip it.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Already started a sign-up and stepped away? Enter the same email again and PlaidCloud picks up where you left off — at the workspace details, the payment page, the status screen, or your finished workspace — rather than telling you the account already exists.
</Aside>

## Name Your Workspace

Enter a company name and a workspace name. PlaidCloud fills in the rest for you: the workspace name is derived from your company, and your region is selected from your location. Both are editable under **Advanced**, where a short note explains where your data will live. Accept the terms of service to continue.

## Choose a Plan

- **Free** is permanent and needs no credit card — accepting creates your workspace right away.
- **Starter** and **Team** include an optional 7-day trial and take you to a secure checkout to enter a card, which is charged when the trial converts. Your receipt comes from our payment provider.

## Watch Your Workspace Build

After you finish the details (or complete checkout), a live status screen takes over. Building a workspace takes a few minutes, and the screen tells you so and keeps you posted as it works — you never land on a dead "check your email" page. The screen refreshes on its own and moves you forward the moment the workspace is ready.

### Invite Your Team While You Wait

The status screen has an **Invite your team** panel. Add up to ten teammates by email and give each one a role:

- **Builder** — can create and run projects, workflows, and dashboards.
- **Viewer** — can open and view what others build.
- **Organization admin** (under **More**) — can also manage billing and workspace settings.

Invitations are held until the workspace is running, then sent automatically — so nobody gets a broken link to a workspace that doesn't exist yet. Need to invite more than ten people, or think of someone later? Add them from the **Members** area of your workspace once you're in.

## Choose Your Password and Sign In

When the workspace is ready, PlaidCloud shows one **Choose your password** screen: confirm a fresh code if asked, set your display name, and pick a password that meets the policy shown. This is the only time you set a password during sign-up.

That password signs you into your workspace, and it's also your password for the control plane — the admin area where billing, plans, and additional workspaces live. You'll rarely need the control plane day to day; when you do, use the same password. If you ever need to change either one, use the **Forgot password** link on that sign-in page.

After you set the password, sign in and you land on your workspace **Home**.

## Land Inside Your Workspace

Home opens with a guided first run:

- A **welcome** and a short tour of where things are.
- A **sample project** to load and run — preselected to match what you told us you wanted to do first — so you reach a finished result and dashboard without building anything.
- A card for bringing in your **own data** by upload or connector, framed as "swap the source, keep the workflow."
- On a paid trial, a **trial banner** showing days remaining and how to upgrade.
- A **help and support** link, and — from your user menu — **Organization & billing**, which opens the control plane for plan and billing tasks.

Teammates you invited receive their welcome email once the workspace is running and follow the same sign-in.

<Aside type="note">
Across a whole sign-up you'll get at most two emails from PlaidCloud — the verification code, and, if the build ran long, a "your workspace is ready" note — plus a payment receipt on paid plans. Both PlaidCloud emails come from the same sender.
</Aside>
