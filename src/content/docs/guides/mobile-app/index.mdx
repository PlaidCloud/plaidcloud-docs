The PlaidCloud app lets you run and monitor your workflows away from your desk.
You can browse your projects, start a workflow or stop one that's running, see
the state of each of its steps, and look back through its run history.

The app is a companion to PlaidCloud in your browser, not a separate product.
You build workflows, tables and dashboards on the web; the app is for keeping
them running.

## Before You Start

- **An existing PlaidCloud workspace and an account in it.** The app has no
  sign-up — your workspace administrator adds you to the workspace on the web,
  as usual.
- **Your workspace name.** It's the first part of the address you use in the
  browser: for `acme.plaid.cloud`, the workspace name is `acme`.
- **A supported device:**

| Device | Minimum version |
| --- | --- |
| iPhone and iPad | iOS or iPadOS 15.1 |
| Android phone or tablet | Android 7.0 |

## Install the App

Search for **PlaidCloud** in your device's app store, or open the listing
directly:

- **iPhone and iPad:** [PlaidCloud on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6769620647)
- **Android:** [PlaidCloud on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.plaidcloud.mobile)

The app is free.

## Sign In

1. Open the app. The first screen asks for your **Workspace**.
2. Type your workspace name, such as `acme`, and tap **Continue**. On iPhone
   and iPad you can also paste the whole workspace address, such as
   `https://acme.plaid.cloud/` — the app keeps only the workspace name.
3. Your workspace's sign-in page opens. It's the same page you see in the
   browser, so sign in the way you normally do — with your password and
   multi-factor code, or with your organization's single sign-on if your
   workspace uses it.
4. When sign-in completes, you're returned to the app.

You stay signed in between sessions; the app renews your sign-in in the
background. Your credentials are entered only on your workspace's own sign-in
page, never in the app itself. The app signs in with OpenID Connect and PKCE
against your workspace, and keeps the resulting tokens in your device's secure
storage.

If sign-in doesn't start, or the app reports that there's no workspace by that
name, check the spelling of the workspace name against the address you use in
the browser.

<Aside type="note">
The app is signed in to one workspace at a time. To use a different workspace,
switch to it from **Settings** (see [Switch Workspace or Sign Out](#switch-workspace-or-sign-out)).
</Aside>

## Find a Workflow

The **Projects** tab lists the projects you have access to, with each
project's description. Pull down on the list to refresh it.

Tap a project to see its workflows, grouped by folder. Each workflow shows its
current state — such as **Running**, **Queued**, **Paused**, **Done**,
**Error** or **Stopped** — its description, and when its last run ended. Use
**Search workflows** at the top to filter the list by workflow name or
description.

On iPhone and iPad, the app also opens on a **Home** tab. **Needs Attention**
lists workflows whose last run failed, with the error message, and **Recent**
lists the projects you've used most recently. Tap either to go straight to it.

## Run and Monitor a Workflow

Tap a workflow to open it. The top of the screen shows its state and a
**Status** panel with when the current or last run **Started** and **Ended**,
its **Duration**, and its latest **Message**. If the workflow is in error, the
error message is shown in red. While a workflow is running or queued, the
screen keeps its state up to date on its own.

The controls at the bottom change with the workflow's state:

| Workflow state | Controls |
| --- | --- |
| Running or queued | **Pause**, **Stop** |
| Anything else | **Run**, **Resume** |

- **Run** starts the workflow from the beginning.
- **Pause** holds a running workflow; **Resume** continues it from where it
  stopped. **Resume** is also how you continue a workflow after fixing the step
  that failed.
- **Stop** ends the run.

Each control asks you to confirm before it acts — for example, **Run this
workflow?**

Below the controls, **Configuration** shows whether the workflow is enabled and
locked and how it handles errors, and **Details** shows its folder and who last
updated it.

<Aside type="note">
The app uses the same permissions as PlaidCloud on the web. If you aren't
allowed to run or change a workflow, the app shows **Action failed** with the
reason, and nothing changes.
</Aside>

## View a Workflow's Steps

Tap **Steps** on a workflow to see every step with its state, its type and its
latest message. A step that won't run shows a **Disabled** badge, and a step
that runs a nested workflow shows a **Sub-workflow** badge.

How the list is laid out depends on the kind of workflow:

- **A workflow built as a graph** shows its steps in run order, with lines on
  the left that trace which steps feed which, including steps that run side by
  side.
- **A workflow built as a list of steps** shows them in order, with the folder
  each step sits in.

## Look Back at Past Runs

Tap **Run history** on a workflow to see its recent runs. Each run shows when
it started, how it finished, how long it took and how many steps it ran.

On iPhone and iPad, tap a run to see any step errors it raised and the last 200
lines of its log, each with its time, level and step.

## Edit Names, Descriptions and Settings

You can make small changes without going back to a computer:

- On a project or workflow, tap **Edit** at the top of the screen.
- On a step, tap the step in the **Steps** list.

The **Edit** sheet lets you change the **Name** and **Description**, and turn
**Enabled** and **Locked** on or off. Tap **Save** to apply your changes or
**Cancel** to discard them. Anything more — changing a step's settings, adding
steps, or rearranging a workflow — is done in PlaidCloud on the web.

## Switch Workspace or Sign Out

Open the **Settings** tab. **Current workspace** shows the workspace you're
signed in to.

- **Switch workspace** signs you out of the current workspace and takes you
  back to the **Workspace** screen to sign in to another.
- **Sign out** signs you out of the workspace.

Both ask you to confirm first.

<Aside type="note">
The current Android release has the **Projects** and **Settings** tabs. The
**Home** tab and the run detail with errors and log lines are in the iPhone and
iPad app.
</Aside>
