Weave, the PlaidCloud AI assistant, is in the app. Ask about your workspace,
find out why a run failed, and approve a fix from your phone. Your
conversations are the same ones you have in PlaidCloud on the web, so you can
start in one place and carry on in the other.

## Before You Start

- **Update the app to version 1.2.0 or later.**
- **Weave must be available to you.** You need the same access to Weave that
  you have on the web. If you don't, the **Weave** tab tells you so.
- **Accept the AI notice the first time.** See [Privacy and Consent](#privacy-and-consent).

## Ask Weave

1. Open the **Weave** tab.
2. Type your question, such as *What failed today?*, and send it.

The answer streams in as Weave writes it. Tap **Stop** to end it early. Tap
the thumbs up or thumbs down under an answer to tell us whether it helped, and
use the share icon to share the answer.

Where Weave names the step, run or workflow behind a statement, tap the link
under the answer to open it.

### Ask From Where You Are

Tap **Ask Weave** on a workflow, a run or a failed step, and Weave starts with
that item as its context. On a failed run, try **Why did this run fail?** or
**How do I fix it?**

### Pick Up a Conversation

Tap the clock icon for your saved conversations. They are shared with
PlaidCloud on the web. From the list you can search, open, rename or delete a
conversation. Deleting it removes it on the web too.

## Approve Weave's Changes

Weave doesn't change anything on its own. When it proposes a change, an
approval card shows what it will do, which items it affects, and whether you
can undo it.

1. Read the card.
2. Tap **Approve** and confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, your fingerprint or your
   device passcode. Changes that can't be undone ask you to confirm first. Tap **Reject** to discard the proposal.

Only after you confirm does Weave make the change, and it reports the
outcome. If something changed since Weave proposed the change, Weave refuses
and asks you to review it again.

PlaidCloud checks your approval on the server, so the card can't be skipped.
The same applies to Weave's changes in PlaidCloud on the web.

## Use Your Voice

- **Dictate a question.** Tap the microphone, speak, and tap it again to stop.
  The text appears in the question box, where you can edit it before you send.
  Speech recognition runs on your device, and your audio never leaves your
  phone. The first time, allow **Microphone** and **Speech Recognition**.
- **Hear the answer.** When you ask by voice, Weave reads a short summary of
  the answer aloud.

## Siri and Shortcuts

<Aside type="note">
Siri works on iPhone and iPad, once you have accepted the AI notice. When your
device is locked, Siri asks you to unlock it first.
</Aside>

- Say **"What failed in PlaidCloud"** and Siri reads a summary of the recent
  failures.
- Say **"Weave in PlaidCloud"** and Siri asks for your question, then reads
  Weave's answer. Siri's answers are read-only: Weave doesn't propose changes
  this way.

Both are also available in the **Shortcuts** app.

On Android, press and hold the app icon and tap **Ask Weave** to open Weave
ready for your question.

## Get Notified

If you leave the app while Weave is still working, a notification tells you
when the answer is ready. Tap it to open the answer. The notification doesn't
contain workflow names or answer text.

<Aside type="note">
Answer notifications are available on iPhone and iPad.
</Aside>

## Weave Findings and the Daily Digest

The **Home** tab shows **Weave findings**, the problems Weave found in your
recent projects that need your attention. Tap one to open it.

To get a daily summary, open **Settings** and turn on **Daily Weave digest**.
It's off by default. The digest is a single notification with counts only.

## Privacy and Consent

The first time you open Weave, a notice titled **Before you use Weave**
explains that your questions, and the workspace information Weave needs to
answer them, are sent to PlaidCloud's AI provider, which processes them on
PlaidCloud's behalf. Tap **Agree and continue** to use Weave. The
[PlaidCloud privacy policy](https://plaidcloud.com/privacy-policy/) names the provider and describes how the data is handled.

To withdraw your consent, open **Settings** and tap **Withdraw AI consent**.
Weave, Siri and notifications for Weave stop until you agree again.
