The member who creates the account is assigned as the owner by default. However, Document accounts are designed to support multiple owners. This feature is helpful when a team is responsible for managing account access or when there is member turnover. Adding and removing owners is similar to adding and removing access permissions.

## Add or Remove Owners

1. Go to `Document > Manage` in PlaidCloud
2. Open the row menu on the account, choose **Who can use it**, then **Owners**
3. Tick the members who should be owners, and clear the ones who should not. Use the filter box to find people, or paste a list of emails
4. Click **Update Owners**

See [Assigning With the Picker](/administration/access/managing-security-groups-and-assignments/#assigning-with-the-picker) for the filter and the other controls.

Because owners are the members who view and edit an account, account administration is set up with two levels:

* The member needs security access to view and manage accounts in general, and
* The member must be an owner of the account to view, manage, and change its settings, unless they are a workspace admin

<Aside>**Manage** shows a member only the accounts they own. A workspace admin sees every document account in **Manage**, owned or not, and can manage any account they can otherwise manage. System-managed accounts are refused for everyone, and a managed-bucket account can't be cloned.</Aside>
