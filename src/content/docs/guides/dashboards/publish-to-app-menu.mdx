You can publish a dashboard, a Panel or Dash app, or a JupyterLite notebook from **Insights** to the app menu on the left. The people you choose then open it from the menu in one click, below the product apps.

<Aside>Publishing to the app menu places an entry in the menu. It never gives anyone access to the item. A person sees the entry only when they are in its audience **and** can already open the item.</Aside>

## Who Can Publish

You need to be able to open the item yourself, and to manage it:

| Item | Who can publish it |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Its owners and workspace admins, the people who choose its audience (see [Control Who Can See a Dashboard](/guides/dashboards/control-who-can-see-a-dashboard/)), with write access to dashboards. |
| Panel or Dash app | Members with write access to apps who have a role above Explorer on the app's project. A locked project doesn't stop you. An app with no project can be published by the person who created it or a workspace admin. |
| JupyterLite notebook | The person who added the deployment, or a workspace admin, with write access to notebooks. |

The **Dashboard Read and Write** security group gives write access to dashboards, apps and notebooks.

## Publish an Item

1. In **Insights**, open **Dashboards**, **Apps** or **Notebooks**.
2. Choose **Publish to app menu…** from the item's row menu, or select the row and choose **Publish to app menu…** in the toolbar. It's offered when you have write access to that kind of item, and on **Dashboards** only for the dashboards you manage.
3. In the **Publish to app menu** dialog, set the entry:
   - **Menu entry** is the label in the menu. It starts as the item's name, and a preview below it shows the entry as it will look. The label can't be wider than the widest product app's name in the menu; a label that is too wide says so.
   - **Icon** offers a monogram of the label's initials or one of 15 symbols, and a color. The first color choice picks one from the label. A small badge on the icon shows whether the entry is a dashboard, an app, a notebook or Jupyter Hub.
   - **Who sees it in the menu** is **Everyone in the workspace**, **Groups** or **People**, one type at a time. For **Groups** or **People**, tick at least one. **Groups** needs access to read security groups, and **People** access to read members; without it, that choice is greyed out.
4. Select **Publish**.

The item's row then shows a **Published** pill. To change the entry later, open **Publish to app menu…** again: the dialog shows what is saved, its button reads **Save**, and **Unpublish** takes the entry out of the menu.

### Who Can't Open It

Under the audience, the dialog checks who in the audience can't open the item today:

- *Can't open this item: 3 of 40* means three of the forty people in the audience won't see the entry until they can open the item.
- *Everyone in this audience can open this item* means nobody is left out.
- *2 people in projects you can't see* counts people whose access depends on projects you can't open, so the dialog can't tell.

For a dashboard, **Fix access** opens its **Who can see this dashboard?** dialog, and the check runs again after you save there. The counts show only when you can see the workspace's members.

## Where the Entry Appears

Published entries come after the product apps in the app menu, below a separator, in the order a workspace admin sets in [Published Apps](#manage-published-apps). Each one has its label and icon. You see a change you make yourself at once; other people see it the next time they load PlaidCloud.

| Item | Opens |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | As a tab inside PlaidCloud |
| **Browser only** Panel app | As a tab inside PlaidCloud |
| JupyterLite notebook | As a tab inside PlaidCloud |
| **Server based** Panel app or Dash app | In a new window |
| Jupyter Hub | In a new window |

A tab opened from the menu has a header with the entry's name, **Reload** and **Open in new window**. If the item doesn't sign you in inside the tab, use **Open in new window**.

A tab's address is a link to it that you can share: see [Sharing Links](/guides/projects/sharing-links/#published-menu-entries). If your app menu holds only **Home** and published entries, **Home** lists them first, in an **Apps** row.

### Who Sees an Entry

A member sees an entry when both of these are true:

- **They are in its audience:** everyone in the workspace, a member of one of its groups, or one of its people.
- **They can open the item:** a dashboard they can see in Insights; an app on a project they can access; and, for a **Browser only** app or a JupyterLite notebook, access to the document account it is served from.

Anyone else doesn't see the entry, whatever its audience.

## Hide an Entry From Your Menu

Right-click a published entry in the app menu and choose **Hide from my menu**. It leaves your menu only, and **Undo** in the message that appears brings it back. Hiding is offered to members who have Insights.

To show it again, open **Hidden apps** in the Insights rail and select **Restore** on its row. An entry that is no longer in your menu reads **No longer in your menu**; **Remove** takes it off the list. The entries you hide are saved with your account, so they stay hidden in every browser.

## Manage Published Apps

Workspace admins open **Published apps** under **Admin** in the Insights rail. It lists every entry in menu order, with its **Name**, **Kind**, **Audience** (**Everyone**, or a number of groups or people) and **Status**. **Opens (30 days)** shows how often each dashboard was opened, for members with usage access.

- **Move up** and **Move down** set the order of the entries in everyone's menu.
- **Change audience…** opens the **Publish to app menu** dialog for an entry you can manage.
- **Unpublish** takes an entry out of the menu for everyone it is published to, after you confirm. Workspace admins can unpublish any entry.

When a published item is deleted, its entry leaves the menu. Removing a Panel app or a JupyterLite deployment from its list removes its entry at the same time. For a deleted dashboard, **Published apps** can show the entry once with **Item deleted, removed**.

## Publish Through the AI Assistant or MCP

`publication_manage` does the same over MCP. Its `kind` is `dashboard`, `panelApp`, `jupyterLite` or `jupyterHub`, and its actions are:

- `get`, `set`, `delete` and `preview` take a `kind` and an `item_id`. `set` replaces the whole entry with a `spec` of `label`, an optional `icon` and `color`, and an `audience` of `type` `public`, `acl-group` or `acl-user` with its `ids`. `preview` takes a `spec` with an `audience` and returns the same counts as the dialog.
- `list` returns the entries you manage, or every entry for a workspace admin, in menu order, for the kinds you can read.
- `reorder` takes `keys` in the new order, each `<kind>:<item_id>`, and is for workspace admins.

The Jupyter Hub link is published only over MCP or the REST API, by a workspace admin, with `kind` `jupyterHub` and `item_id` `hub`. `publication_manage` is not available to Weave or to other AI agents acting for you.
