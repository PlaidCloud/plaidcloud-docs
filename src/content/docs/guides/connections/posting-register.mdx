The **Posting Register** lists every document posted to your ERP systems, across every project and system you're allowed to see. It opens on **Needs attention**, the documents a person has to act on, and from there you can open any posting, see why it is where it is, and act on it.

Documents reach the register from the **Post Documents** step, from each ERP's own posting steps (such as **Intacct: Post Journal Entry**), from a posting file, and from the reverse, credit and correct actions described below. How a run is started, checked and sent is covered on [Post Documents to ERP Systems](/guides/connections/post-documents/); how a system is set up is covered on [ERP Systems](/guides/connections/erp-systems/).

## Open the Register

Open the **Posting Register** app. It appears for any member who holds one of the `erp.register.*` permissions described in [Who Can See What](#who-can-see-what). It always opens on **Needs attention**.

The rail down the left has three groups:

| Group | Pages |
|---|---|
| **Posting** | **Needs attention**, **Postings**, **Runs** |
| **Needs me** | **Approvals**, **Delegations**, **My watches** |
| **Settings** | **Systems**, **Hold rules**, **History of changes**, **Exports & alerts**, **Retention** |

**Needs attention** and **Approvals** show a count on the rail. **Retention** is hidden unless you hold `erp.register.admin`. **Runs** opens the list of posting runs, and **Systems** and **Hold rules** are explained on [Post Documents to ERP Systems](/guides/connections/post-documents/) and [ERP Systems](/guides/connections/erp-systems/).

You can also reach the register from elsewhere in PlaidCloud:

- **Home** shows a **Posting Register** card while anything needs you, with **Needs attention** and **Waiting for approval** counts. Choosing it opens **Needs attention**.
- The command palette lists **Needs attention** and **Approvals** with their counts. Type a ledger id and choose **Find posting** followed by the id to open that posting.

### Live Updates

The register keeps itself current. When postings change on a system, the rail counts are read again, an open list shows **Postings have changed since this loaded** with a **Refresh** button instead of moving rows under you, and an open posting reloads in place.

## Who Can See What

### Permissions

Four permissions open and act in the register. Holding one does not grant another, but every action that needs `erp.register.operate`, `erp.register.approve` or `erp.register.admin` also needs `erp.register.view`. Every action checks the permissions it needs on the server, whatever the screen shows.

| Permission | What it allows |
|---|---|
| `erp.register.view` | Open the register; read **Needs attention**, **Postings** and a posting's details; ask a question on **Postings**; read comments and sign-offs. |
| `erp.register.operate` | **Reverse…**, **Credit…**, **Correct…** and **Download as a posting file…**; **Settle from stored reports**; **Say whether it posted** (with `erp.register.approve` as well); comment, sign off and watch; open **Approvals**, withdraw your own requests and, with approval authority, decide others'; see or revoke delegations; downloads, schedules and the evidence export. |
| `erp.register.approve` | Approve reversals; read and decide **Held documents**; delegate your approvals; say whether an **Unconfirmed** posting went through. |
| `erp.register.admin` | Exports and the evidence export in the auditor profile; manage another member's schedule; set **Retention**; change [hold rules](/guides/connections/erp-systems/). |

Four built-in security groups bundle these: **Posting Register Viewer** (view), **Posting Register Operator** (adds operate), **Posting Register Approver** (adds approve) and **Posting Register Admin** (all four).

A few actions also need a project permission rather than a register one: **Retry** and **Check in the system** need `analyze.workflow.write`, and **Retry** also needs an Architect or Manager role in the posting's project. **History of changes** needs `analyze.connection.read`.

The same permissions and visibility rules apply when you use the register through the REST API or the MCP tools: each route and tool runs the same checks as the screen.

### Which Postings You See

Holding a register permission opens the register, but it doesn't by itself show you any posting. A posting is visible to you only when all of these hold:

- **You hold a personal grant on its system's connection.** You must own the connection, be named on it, or be in a security group named on it — see the connection's [Security Model](/guides/connections/create-connection/#security-model). A connection shared with **All Workspace Members** does not count, and neither does a service account's grant.
- **You can open its project**, when it belongs to one.
- **The project lets you see its entity.** When a project limits members to certain entities through row access on `entity`, you see only those entities' postings. A project Architect is never limited.

Your access is read again on every request, so a revoked grant takes effect the next time a page loads. A member with no connection grants sees **No systems to show** instead of an empty list.

## The Seven States

Every document, whatever posted it, reads as one of seven states. The same words appear on every pill, chip and filter.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Would post** | Checked. Its system, or the run, is in Simulate, so nothing was sent. |
| **Needs approval** | A rule holds it for a person to approve. |
| **Waiting** | Approved, and about to be sent or sent. The system has not answered yet. |
| **Posted** | The ERP accepted this posting. |
| **Not posted** | A check stopped it, or the system rejected it. The reason says which. |
| **Unconfirmed** | No answer came, and the system cannot be asked. Say whether it posted. |
| **Reversed** | A later posting canceled this one. |

## Choose What You're Looking At

**Needs attention** and **Postings** share one scope, so a filter you set on one is in effect on the other. The bar under the page header shows it: anything you've narrowed is a chip you can remove, and anything left at its default is stated in words, such as **All systems**.

Choose **Change scope** to edit it:

- **Posted** — **This month**, **Last month**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Custom**, which asks for **From** and **To** dates. The default is the last 30 days.
- **System** and **Project**, picked from the ones you can see.
- **Entity**, under **Advanced ▾**.

**Reset** puts the scope back to its default. Under the bar, a row of **State** chips narrows the list to one state.

The browser address carries the scope, so you can bookmark it or send it to a colleague. Whoever opens it sees only what their own access allows.

## Needs Attention

**Needs attention** lists every document a person has to act on, approvals first, then **Unconfirmed**, then **Not posted**, then posted documents that need a look. Selecting a row opens it. The line under the page name counts what needs attention and how many of those wait for approval, for example "12 need attention · 3 to approve".

Each row shows **Reference**, **Entity**, **System**, **Amount**, **Currency**, **State**, **Why**, **Next** and **Mode**; **Project** and **Type** are hidden columns you can show. **Why** is the first reason in plain words, with "(and 2 more)" when there are others, and **Next** is what to do about it.

**Mode** says **Live** or **Simulated**. Simulated documents need a person too — a failed check has to be fixed before you post them — so this page shows both unless a link names one mode.

The **State** chips here are **Needs approval**, **Not posted**, **Unconfirmed** and **Posted**. When nothing matches, the page shows **All clear**: "Nothing needs a person. Every document is posted, waiting, or would post." If your scope is narrowed, it offers **Reset scope**.

### Why a Document Needs Attention

A **Not posted** document carries the reason it stopped. Reasons that come from a check, before anything is sent, are fixed at the source or in the system, then run again (see [Post Documents to ERP Systems](/guides/connections/post-documents/)). For most of them **Next** reads **Fix and run again**; for a document with no system for its entity it reads **Retry**, which routes the document again once you add one; and for a reference that contains `~` or is too long, **Next** is blank:

| Reason, as shown | What to do |
|---|---|
| No system is set to post for the entity | Choose a system for it under **Systems**. |
| Its system is retired | Post to another system. |
| The period is closed in the system | Open the period, or post to the next one. |
| An account does not exist in the system | Create it there, or match the code to another. |
| A code has no match in the system | Match it under **Systems**. |
| An invoice this payment pays is not open | Check the invoice in the system. |
| It is for another company than its system posts to | Route it to that company's system. |
| A line is in a currency its system does not book | Post it in the system's currency, or to another system. |
| The system needs a value that is empty | Fill it in at the source. |
| Debits and credits do not match | Fix the source and run again. |
| Part of its set is missing, or the documents of its set do not agree | A set posts only when every document is there and they agree. |
| The reference was already posted to another system, or is already used on this system | Fix it, then run again. |
| Its reference contains `~`, or is too long for the system | Change the reference at the source. PlaidCloud keeps `~` for numbering versions. |
| This system cannot take this kind of document yet | Fix it, then run again. |
| An earlier version of this reference has no answer from the system yet | Run again once it has one. |

Other reasons describe what happened after the checks passed, and **Next** offers [**Retry**](#retry) for them: an approver declined it, the run was stopped before it was sent, the agent for the system was offline, it was not sent because something failed before it reached the system, the system rejected it, or the system did not have it when PlaidCloud looked at the deadline.

The rest need attention without being **Not posted**:

| Reason | State | Next |
|---|---|---|
| It needs a person to approve it, naming the rule that holds it | **Needs approval** | **Approve** — see [Approvals](#approvals). |
| The system did not answer in time, and it cannot be asked whether the document posted | **Unconfirmed** | **Say whether it posted** — see [Unconfirmed Postings](#unconfirmed-postings). |
| The source changed after this was posted | **Posted** | **Correct with this version** or **Keep the posted one**, answered on the run page. |
| Only part of its set posted | **Posted** | **Fix and run again** |
| Posted, but it is not in the system's own record, or the record differs | **Posted** | **Check in the system** |
| Changed in the system after it was posted | **Posted** | **Check in the system** |
| Entered in the system without going through PlaidCloud | **Posted** | **Check in the system** |

Entries made directly in a system, without PlaidCloud, are grouped into one row per project, system, entity and month, such as "4 entries in October 2026 not made by PlaidCloud". These rows are listed only when no filter narrows the list, and they aren't included in the count at the top. The **Posted** dates count as a filter, and the scope starts at the last 30 days, so these rows show once you choose **Custom** under **Posted** in **Change scope** and leave **From** and **To** empty.

## Postings

**Postings** lists every document you can see, one row per document: **Reference**, **Type**, **Entity**, **System**, **Project**, **Amount**, **Currency**, **State**, **System document**, **Posting date**, **Started by**, **Approved** and **Mode**. **System document** is the number the system gave it, followed by **In the books** once a check found it in the system's own record. **Approved** says **Automatically** or **By a person**. The line under the page name counts the postings and totals them per currency; currencies are never added together.

In the header:

- The search box finds a reference, system document or amount as you type.
- **Ask** takes a question in your own words, typed in the same search box, such as "What did we post in October?". It understands states, entities, the mode and months, and can only narrow what you may see. The list then shows the first 25 matches with a sentence saying what was understood.
- **Live**, **Simulated** and **All** choose which postings to show. **Live** is the default.

The **State** chips cover all seven states. A long list stops at 1,000 rows with a notice; **Load more** reads the next 1,000.

Tick one or more rows, then use the toolbar's **Actions** menu for **Reverse…**, **Credit…**, **Correct…** or **Download as a posting file…** on all of them at once. These need `erp.register.operate`, and work as described under [Reverse, Credit or Correct](#reverse-credit-or-correct). Rows entered in a system without PlaidCloud can't be acted on here.

## The Posting Drawer

Open a posting from its row's magnifier icon, or by selecting a row on **Needs attention**. The posting opens in a drawer on the right, beside the list.

The top of the drawer shows the state pill, then each reason in plain words with the system's own message under it, then the posting's facts: **Reference**, **Type**, **Amount**, **System document**, **System**, **Environment**, **Entity**, **Posting date**, **Project**, **Workflow**, **Step**, **Started by**, **Approved** and **Mode**. A reversal waiting for approval also says "This reversal needs a second person to approve it. Whoever started it cannot."

Five tabs hold the rest:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Lines** | Each line's **Account**, **Description**, **Debit**, **Credit** and **Dimensions**. |
| **History** | What happened to the document, when and by whom. |
| **Versions** | Every version of the reference, such as `INV-100` and `INV-100~2`, with its state and which one you're looking at. |
| **Links** | Where it came from and what it relates to: **Source rows**, **Step version**, **Step last changed by**, **Reverses**, **Reversed by**, **Credits** and any **Attachment**. |
| **Sign-off** | The review sign-off and the comments on the posting — see [Sign-off and Comments](#sign-off-and-comments). |

The drawer's **Actions** menu holds **Copy link**, which copies a link that opens this posting for anyone allowed to see it, and, where they apply, **Open in the system** (for QuickBooks Online postings, which can be opened directly), **Watch connection** (when the posting has a connection) and **Watch run** (when it has a run). Members with `erp.register.operate` also get **Credit…**, **Correct…**, **Download as a posting file…** and **Check in the system** there, and **Say whether it posted** and **Reverse…** as buttons beside it. Without `erp.register.operate`, the footer says "Reversing needs the Posting Register Operator role." **Retry** appears beside the menu for anyone it applies to — see [Retry](#retry).

Each action is offered only where the posting's state allows it, and a grayed-out item says why when you point at it.

### Retry

**Retry** appears when **Next** offers it, the document has a ledger entry, and you hold `analyze.workflow.write`. It first checks what would be sent; if nothing can be retried it says why. Otherwise it asks "Send the stored document again to" the system, and on **Retry** the stored copy goes out again as a new run, under the project's posting limits.

A document is retried only when it is certain the system didn't book it: **Not posted**, or never sent. You also need an Architect or Manager role in its project and your own grant on its connection.

### Reverse, Credit or Correct

Only a **Posted** document can be reversed, credited or corrected:

- **Reverse…** undoes the posting in its system.
- **Credit…** issues a credit memo against a posted invoice. It applies to customer and supplier invoices only. When you credit a single posting, leave **Amount** blank to credit the whole invoice, or enter part of it.
- **Correct…** reverses the current version and, when PlaidCloud holds a newer version of the document that hasn't posted, sends it as the next version (`REF~2`). It is also offered on a posting whose source changed after it posted.

Each opens a dialog that first checks what can be done: how many postings will be acted on, in which system, and how — "the system's own reversal", "a credit memo" or "an opposite posting" — and which can't, with a reason for each. Reversing a document that posted as part of a set takes the rest of the set with it. Give a **Reason**, optionally change the **Date** (it starts on today), and confirm; the button counts the postings, such as **Reverse 3**.

The action then runs like any posting: it is recorded as a new run, checked, approved if a rule holds it, sent and confirmed, and the run page opens. One action can carry at most 200 postings.

Reverse, credit and correct are live writes, so a posting is refused when its project is capped at Simulate, when its project isn't authorized to post to that system, or when you hold no personal grant on its connection. Some systems have no way to undo a posting through their interface; the dialog says so for those.

### Segregation of Duties

When segregation of duties is turned on for your workspace, a reversal at or above its threshold needs a second person's approval. Segregation of duties and its threshold are set for your workspace by PlaidCloud; there is no setting for them in the register.

- The document reads **Needs approval** in the run, and an approval request goes to every approver who holds a grant on that posting's connection. There's no one to address it to: the connection grant is the routing.
- The approver must hold `erp.register.approve`, or an active delegation from someone who does, and their own grant on the connection. Whoever requested the reversal can't approve it, even through a delegation.
- An approval covers exactly that reversal: the posting, its reason and its date. Once it is approved and its five-minute hold has passed, run the same action again with the same reason and date, and it goes through.
- A submitter's reversals on the same connection over the last 24 hours count toward the threshold together, so a large reversal can't be split into smaller ones to stay under it.
- When fewer than two members qualify to approve on a connection, reversals that need approval are refused.

### Download as a Posting File

**Download as a posting file…** asks for an **Action** (**Reverse**, **Credit** or **Correct**) and a **Format** (**Excel** or **CSV**), then saves a posting file for that action, filled in. Edit the amounts or lines, then post it from the file through the REST API or the MCP tools — see [Post Documents to ERP Systems](/guides/connections/post-documents/).

### Check in the System

**Check in the system** looks the posting up in its system now, and never simulates. It says **Found in the system.** or **Not found in the system.**, or why the system couldn't be asked, and the posting's **In the books** status updates. It needs `analyze.workflow.write`.

### Sign-off and Comments

The **Sign-off** tab records a review of the posting with **Mark reviewed**, **Mark cleared** or **Needs follow-up**, and an optional **Note**. It shows the current sign-off and every earlier one. 

Below it, **Comments** holds plain-text notes about the posting. Type a comment, list any member ids to mention, and choose **Post comment**. A mentioned member is notified only if they can see the posting when the notification is sent.

Comments and sign-off need `erp.register.operate` to add and `erp.register.view` to read. A document that has no ledger entry yet says "Comments and sign-off are not available for this document yet."

### Watch

**Watch connection** and **Watch run** in the drawer's **Actions** menu notify you of activity on the posting's connection or run from then on; they read **Watching connection** and **Watching run** once on. A notification goes to you only while you can still see the posting it is about. Watching needs `erp.register.operate`.

## Unconfirmed Postings

A posting is **Unconfirmed** when its system didn't answer in time and can't be asked whether the document posted. A person settles it from what they find in the system.

### Say Whether It Posted

Open the posting and choose **Say whether it posted**. Look for the document in its system, then choose:

- **It posted**, and give its **Document** number in the system. The posting becomes **Posted** with that number.
- **It did not post**. The posting becomes **Not posted** and can be retried.

A **Note** saying what you saw is required. A wrong answer can post a document twice or record one the system never got, so this needs `erp.register.operate`, `erp.register.approve` and your own grant on the connection; without `erp.register.approve`, the button is grayed out and says so. If the system's own answer arrives first, its answer stands.

### Settle From Stored Reports

SAP postings leave a report of their run. On **Needs attention**, **Settle from stored reports** appears in the toolbar while something is **Unconfirmed** and you hold `erp.register.operate`. It reads the reports for the checked rows, or for every system in your scope when none are checked, says how many documents will be settled to **Posted** and lists those it can't settle, with why. Choose the button that counts them, such as **Settle 3 documents**, to apply it.

Settling from a report only ever moves a document to **Posted**; nothing is marked rejected. A document whose report is missing, older than the run that sent it, or doesn't show it posted stays **Unconfirmed**.

## Approvals

**Approvals** is where you decide what waits on you. Chips in the header switch between:

- **Waiting on me** — reversal approval requests you can decide.
- **Submitted by me** — the requests you made.
- **Held documents** — reversals, credits and corrections waiting for a second person, and documents a hold rule is holding, shown only if you hold `erp.register.approve`.

The **Delegations** link in the header opens [Delegations](#delegations).

### Reversal Requests

Each request shows the entry, its amount and entity, who asked and how long ago, the period it reverses into, and the requester's reason. Choose **Approve** or **Reject**, give the reason recorded with your decision, and confirm. To approve several at once, tick them and choose the header's **Approve selected (3)** button, which counts the rows you ticked.

- After you approve, the message offers **Undo** for 8 seconds, and the row offers **Undo approval** while the five-minute hold lasts. Once the hold passes, the reversal may already be in the system.
- On **Submitted by me**, **Withdraw** takes back a request that is still waiting or approved.
- A request that has waited four hours shows **Escalated**, and after 24 hours **Escalated twice**. One that is never decided expires after seven days and must be asked for again.

If you hold no approval authority, the page says **You cannot approve reversals** instead of listing requests.

### Held Documents

**Held documents** lists the reversals, credits and corrections waiting for a second person first, then, oldest first, the documents a [hold rule](/guides/connections/erp-systems/) is holding. They are grouped by run. A document flagged as an unusual size shows **Unusual**.

Choose **Approve** to send a document, or the button on a run's card that counts its documents, such as **Approve all 3 documents**, for every document it shows, after a confirmation naming the systems they go to. **Decline** asks why, and makes the document **Not posted**. An approved document is sent under the run's mode and the project's current limits and authorizations.

You can't decide a document from a run you started or sent, or one that acts on a posting you made: someone else has to approve it. A set is approved whole, so a set with any document you can't decide is left for someone who can.

## Delegations

**Delegations** lets an approver hand their approval authority to another member for a while, such as when they will be away, and shows any authority handed to you. Two cards list **You delegated** and **Delegated to you**.

Choose **Delegate approvals**, pick a **Member**, set the number of **Days** (seven by default, at most 30), and give a **Reason**, which is recorded on the audit trail. **Revoke** ends a delegation early; either side can revoke it.

- Delegating needs `erp.register.approve`. Seeing and revoking a delegation need only `erp.register.operate`, because the person you delegate to usually doesn't hold the approver permission — that's why you delegate to them. They must hold `erp.register.operate`.
- You can't delegate to yourself, to someone who has delegated to you, or to a service account, and a delegate can't pass the authority on.
- A delegate can't approve a request made by the person who delegated to them. While a delegate approves, the **Approvals** header says whose authority they're using, and each decision is recorded as made under that approver's authority.

## My Watches

**My watches** lists the connections and runs you're watching. Choose **Watch a connection** to add one of the connections you can see, and **Stop watching** to remove one; **Undo** on the message watches it again.

## Exports & Alerts

**Exports & alerts** is where the register leaves PlaidCloud as a file or a notification. Everything here needs `erp.register.operate`; without it, the page explains that you can read the register but not export it.

<Aside type="caution">
A downloaded file leaves PlaidCloud. Grants, roles and masking are checked every time a file is built, including at each scheduled send, but nothing is checked after delivery. Free-text fields are excluded from every export.
</Aside>

### What a File Contains

Every file is built from your own access at the moment it is made, with one of two profiles:

- **Standard** — amounts, currency and the member who posted each document are withheld.
- **Auditor** — amounts, currency and the member who posted each document are included. This profile needs `erp.register.admin`.

The page tells you which profile your exports use.

### Download Now

From the toolbar (or its **Actions** menu):

- **Download CSV** and **Download JSON** export the register. With an export schedule selected, the file uses that schedule's filter.
- **Evidence export…** builds one period's evidence — see [Evidence Export](#evidence-export).

### Schedules and Alerts

Choose **New schedule** and fill in:

- **Name** and **Kind**: **Export file** or **Alert**.
- **Format** (CSV or JSON) for an export file, or **Send when** for an alert: **A live posting fails**, **An authorization is aging** or **A reversal is posted**.
- **Cadence**: **On demand**, **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**.
- **Recipients**: PlaidCloud members, never email addresses.
- Optionally a **Connection** and an **Entity** to narrow it.

A daily job sends every **Daily**, **Weekly** and **Monthly** schedule whose interval has passed since it last sent. **On demand** schedules send only when you choose **Send Now**. **Delete Schedule** stops a schedule for good.

Each recipient gets what their own access allows at the moment of sending, so two recipients of one schedule can receive different files. A recipient who no longer holds the export role, holds no connection grants, has no email address or is disabled is skipped, and **Send Now** names who was skipped and why. Only a schedule's owner, or a member with `erp.register.admin`, can send or delete it.

### Evidence Export

**Evidence export…** saves one period in five parts: what was posted, what is in the books, who approved, what changed in the rules and logic, and who could do what. Enter the **Period** (such as `2026-10`), optionally one or more entities under **Entity** (blank is all), choose **Excel** or **JSON**, and choose **Download**.

The file covers live postings dated in the period that you can see. The standard profile withholds money and every person — who started, requested, decided and changed things, the approvers' reasons, the rules' before and after, and the access section — so the auditor profile is the one that carries the full record.

## History of Changes

**History of changes** lists every change to a system's settings, a system's retirement, an SAP function, a code match, which entity posts where, a hold rule, Go live and list copies, with **When**, **By**, **What**, **System**, **Which**, **From** and **To**. It shows up to 2,000 changes; narrow it to one system to see the rest. It needs `analyze.connection.read`.

## Retention

**Retention**, under **Settings**, sets how long the register keeps three kinds of history: **Capture (sidecar request/response)**, **Simulation store** and **Write audit trail**. Each is a number of days, up to 3,650, or **Unlimited**. Choose **Save** to apply them. The card says who last set the policy and when; until someone does, every horizon is unlimited.

Only members with `erp.register.admin` see **Retention** on the rail and can change it. A daily sweep applies the policy.

<Aside type="note">
Retention never reaches the record of what was posted, which is what stops a document from being posted twice. Past the capture horizon, a posting's captured request and response are cleared, but the posting, its state and its details stay. Past the simulation horizon, simulated postings are deleted.
</Aside>
