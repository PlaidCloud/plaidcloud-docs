[Data Foundation](/guides/projects/data-foundation/) brings data from every ERP and accounting system you connect, in as many instances as you have, into one Common Finance Model. QuickBooks Online is one of the systems it reads directly. This page is for the QuickBooks administrator who sets up the access, and for the PlaidCloud user who asks them to.

PlaidCloud only reads data. Nobody sends a password or a token by email.

## Send the Access Request

On the project's **Systems** page, under **Data Foundation** in **Home**, choose your system in **System** under **Add a system** and click **Don't have access yet?**. The system waits for access, and one request covering the project's systems is shown. Click **Copy request** to paste it into your own message, or **Email request** to open it in your mail program with the subject "Access for PlaidCloud."

## What the QuickBooks Administrator Sets Up

<Steps>

1. **Choose the QuickBooks user PlaidCloud will act as.** In QuickBooks itself, that user needs to see:

   - the Chart of Accounts, Customers, Vendors, Products and Services, Locations and Classes
   - the General Ledger, Trial Balance and Profit and Loss reports (a user who can run them under Reports)
   - sales transactions: invoices, sales receipts, credit memos and refund receipts
   - purchases and payments, if you want suppliers, open items and payments in the model: bills, vendor credits, expenses, customer payments and bill payments
   - budgets and the Inventory Valuation Summary, if you want them in the model (QuickBooks Plus or Advanced)

   A company admin already has this. A limited user needs those permissions turned on.

2. **Authorize PlaidCloud once.** That user signs in on Intuit's consent screen, from the connection the PlaidCloud user sends them. The scope is Accounting (`com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting`) only.

3. **One company per connection.** If you keep several QuickBooks companies, authorize each one on its own connection.

</Steps>

## What to Send Back

| The administrator sends | Entered in PlaidCloud as |
|---|---|
| The company's name, as it should appear in the model | Nothing to enter: the connection reads the one company the QuickBooks user approves |
| Whether the books are closed to a date (**Settings > Account and Settings > Advanced > Accounting**) | Nothing to enter: PlaidCloud reads the close-the-books date from QuickBooks |

On the project's **Systems** page, the PlaidCloud user chooses QuickBooks Online in **System** under **Add a system** and clicks **Connect to** and the system's name. PlaidCloud makes the connection for the project's environment and opens Intuit's approval window. Once the QuickBooks user approves, the system is added and starts loading. See [Connect Your ERP](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#connect-your-erp).

Under **System settings**, **Retained earnings account** names the Equity account the opening profit and loss rolls into, when the account names don't already say. It's optional.

## Check the Access

Back in PlaidCloud, once the system is added, click **Test connection** on its row on the **Systems** page. It reads the required datasets (entities, chart of accounts, fiscal calendar, trial balance and general ledger) and shows each as **Readable**, **Missing permission** or **Error**. The optional ones aren't part of the test: if QuickBooks refuses one, the readiness checks name it after the next load. What each dataset reads:

| Dataset | What it reads in QuickBooks |
|---|---|
| Entities | The company information |
| Chart of accounts | The Chart of Accounts |
| Fiscal calendar | The company preferences: first month of the fiscal year and the close-the-books date |
| Trial balance | The Trial Balance report, for opening balances |
| General ledger | The General Ledger report |
| Sites | Locations (optional) |
| Profit centers | Classes (optional) |
| Customers | Customers (optional) |
| Products | Products and Services (optional) |
| Suppliers | Vendors and their terms (optional) |
| Month-end balance | The Trial Balance report at each month end (optional) |
| Bank balances | The bank accounts' balances in that month-end Trial Balance (optional) |
| Sales lines | Invoices, sales receipts, credit memos and refund receipts (optional) |
| Supplier invoice lines | Bills, vendor credits and expenses (optional) |
| Open items | Invoices, credit memos, bills and vendor credits, less the payments applied to them, at each month end (optional) |
| Payments | Customer payments and bill payments (optional) |
| Budget | Budgets (optional) |
| Inventory balances | The Inventory Valuation Summary at each month end, when the company tracks quantity on hand (optional) |
| Currency rates | Exchange rates (optional) |

When a dataset shows **Missing permission**, the test names the access to add. Ask the administrator to turn it on for the user and test again. A company without the optional data, such as budgets, still loads everything else. When QuickBooks refuses a read that a month's balances, inventory, sales, purchases, open items or payments are built from, that month keeps the rows it already had and is read again at the next load.

QuickBooks has no trading partner, intercompany account or ledger, so a QuickBooks company's lines carry none. It has no departments either: its locations are sites and its classes profit centers.
