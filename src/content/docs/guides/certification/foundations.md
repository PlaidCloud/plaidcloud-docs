## What the Lab Asks

The lab is called *Foundations: sales by region*.

Orders and customers are already loaded in the lab project. In its workflow, you build a step that keeps only the completed orders, joins them to customers, and writes one row per region with the total amount and the number of orders. Then you run the workflow and publish the result table.

The lab checks that the workflow runs and that the result table holds the right totals.
