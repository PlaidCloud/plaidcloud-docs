The **Cost to Serve** template shows what it costs to serve each customer and what margin is left after it. It is built on the [Common Finance Model](/guides/projects/data-foundation/), so it works from whichever ERPs feed the project, and it includes everything **Enterprise Profitability** builds.

## How It Works

1. Operating expense is pooled by entity, period and the department it is booked to.
2. Each pool takes the activity and driver that your **activity map** names for its department.
3. Each customer's drivers come from the project's invoiced sales lines.
4. Each pool is split to customers by its driver, within entity and period.

A pool whose driver has no positive value for that entity and period passes through with no customer, so the split always adds up to the pool.

## The Activity Map

The activity map is the table `cts_activity_map`. It is yours to edit, and the model never writes to it. Each row gives a department an **activity** and a **driver**.

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `department` | A department code, or `*` for every department not listed. |
| `activity` | The name of the activity, such as Warehousing. |
| `driver` | What to split that department's cost by (see below). |

| Driver | Splits cost by |
|---|---|
| `orders` | Each customer's number of orders (distinct invoices). |
| `lines` | Invoice lines. |
| `quantity` | Units handled. A return is handled too, so it counts by its size. |
| `revenue` | Net revenue, with credit memos netted. |

The sample data ships with departments `SALES`, `SERVICE` and `OPS`, and a `*` row for the rest. Change them to your own departments and activities, then click **Apply changes** on the **Foundation** tab.

## What You Get

| Output | Holds |
|---|---|
| `cts_by_customer` | By entity, period, customer and activity: the cost to serve, and the customer's revenue, cost of goods sold, gross margin, net margin after cost to serve and orders. |
| `cts_per_order` | Cost to serve per order. |
| `cts_tie_out` | The ledger's operating expense against the cost spread to customers, by entity and period. The difference is 0 when nothing was lost or counted twice. |

Metrics for the assistant and dashboards: **Cost to Serve**, **Net margin after CTS**, **Orders** and **CTS per order**, by entity, customer or activity. Cost with the activity `Unmapped`, or with no customer, is operating expense that no activity or driver could place.

## Start a Project

<Steps>

1. In Analyze, open **Projects** and click **New Project**.

2. Under **Start from**, choose **Cost to Serve (template)**. Tick **Start with sample data** to see a worked example first.

3. Connect your ERPs and run **Load**. See [Get Started With Data Foundation](/guides/projects/data-foundation-getting-started/).

4. Edit the activity map for your departments and click **Apply changes**.

</Steps>

Save your activity map with the project by using **Actions > Save as my template...**. See [Save as My Template](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#save-as-my-template).
