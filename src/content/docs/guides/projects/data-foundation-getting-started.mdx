This walkthrough takes you from a new project to a loaded, checked model that reports, metrics and AI assistants read, and then hands it over. It follows the eight items on the **Setup** checklist of the project's **Foundation** tab, plus one step to name things the way your business does. Each step links to the part of [Data Foundation: The Common Finance Model](/guides/projects/data-foundation/) that covers it in full.

## Before You Start

- You need to be able to create projects, and for a shared project, to be its Architect.
- Have the credentials for each ERP you want to connect. If you don't hold them, the **Foundation** tab writes an access request for your ERP administrator. See [Connect Your ERP](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#connect-your-erp).
- Decide which template fits. **Enterprise Profitability** builds the profit and loss and margin bridges. **Cost to Serve** adds the cost of serving each customer. **Operational Transfer Pricing** tests each entity's profit against your policy ranges. All three read the same Common Finance Model. See [Build on the Model With a Template](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#build-on-the-model-with-a-template).

## Walk Through the Setup Checklist

<Steps>

1. **Create the project.** In Analyze, open **Projects** and click **New Project**. Under **Start from**, choose a template. To try the model before you connect anything, tick **Start with sample data**: the project runs a first **Load** straight away and the **Foundation** tab shows a complete example. To reuse a model you or a colleague saved, choose **From a template file...**. See [Save as My Template](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#save-as-my-template).

2. **Connect your data.** On the **Foundation** tab, pick an existing connection or create one, click **Test connection**, tick the entities to load, set **Months of history** and click **Connect**. To bring in another system or another instance, repeat this and tick **Add this as another source instead of replacing the current one**. Add a file for data outside your ERPs. See [Connect Your ERP](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#connect-your-erp) and [Sources](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#sources).

3. **Load your data.** Click **Load**. It conforms every source into the model, translates it into your reporting currency and checks it. **What to do next** lists what needs your attention, most important first. See [Load Your Data](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#load-your-data).

4. **Answer the questions the load raises.** Where sources use the same code for different things, **Codes waiting for your answer** asks whether they mean the same. Where two sources cover the same months, **Choose which source counts from when**. Click **Apply changes** to rebuild without pulling from the ERPs again. Instead of answering one by one, you can ask the assistant to propose answers. See [Map Codes Across Sources](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#map-codes-across-sources) and [Resolve Open Questions With Ask](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#resolve-open-questions-with-ask).

5. **Check that totals tie out.** The **Readiness** grid shows one row per entity, period and check. A figure you doubt can be opened with **Explain this number**, which splits it into what the ledger holds, what was allocated in and out, and what was eliminated in the group. See [Readiness Checks](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#readiness-checks) and [Explain a Number](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#explain-a-number).

6. **Review your hierarchies.** Click **Open hierarchies**, check the Entity, Account and Department hierarchies, then tick **I have reviewed them**. See [Hierarchies](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#hierarchies).

7. **Name things the way your business does.** Under **Business names**, give categories, P&L lines, metrics and columns the names your business uses. Reports, metrics and the assistant show them. See [Business Names](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#business-names). If the model's cost spreading doesn't match how you work, change it in **Actions > Allocations...**. See [Allocation Rules](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#allocation-rules).

8. **Keep it current.** Once a load succeeds, click **Refresh nightly**. Optionally set up a [Board Pack](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#board-pack), which writes an Excel workbook and a PDF of the profit and loss dashboard to a folder you choose, on demand or on a schedule you give its workflow.

9. **Share, document and hand over.** Share the project with your team, click **Download evidence pack** for a record of what was loaded and how it ties out, and, if you set the project up for a client, make their owner an Architect and then remove yourself. See [Hand the Project Over](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#hand-the-project-over).

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Before a large change to sources or mappings, you don't need to copy anything first: a restore point is made automatically, and **Actions > Restore...** puts the project back. See [Restore Points](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#restore-points).
</Aside>

## What You Have at the End

- A Common Finance Model with one chart of accounts, one set of entities and one reporting currency, and a management group profit and loss with intercompany eliminated.
- A model, metrics and an analysis path your team and the assistant can ask questions of.
- A nightly refresh that also makes your board pack, if you set one up.
- An evidence pack and a project your client's owner administers.
