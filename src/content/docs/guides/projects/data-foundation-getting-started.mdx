This walkthrough takes you from a new project to a loaded, checked model that reports, metrics and AI assistants read, and then hands it over. At each stage, **Summary**, under **Data Foundation** in the project's **Home** rail, offers the one next step as a button, such as **Add your finance system**, **Load**, **Answer 3 questions** or **Open the reports**. Each step below links to the part of [Data Foundation: The Common Finance Model](/guides/projects/data-foundation/) that covers it in full.

## Before You Start

- You need to be able to create projects, and for a shared project, to be its Architect.
- Have the credentials for each system you want to connect. If you don't hold them, **Don't have access yet?** writes an access request for your administrator. See [Connect Your ERP](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#connect-your-erp).
- Decide whether you need a solution. **Data Foundation** on its own gives you the model and its reports. **Enterprise Profitability** allocates costs to products and customers. **Cost to Serve** adds the cost of serving each customer. **Operational Transfer Pricing** tests each entity's profit against your policy ranges. Each solution includes Data Foundation and reads the same Common Finance Model. See [Build on the Model With a Template](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#build-on-the-model-with-a-template).

## Walk Through the Setup

<Steps>

1. **Create the project.** In Analyze, open **Projects** and click **New Project**. Under **Start from**, choose **Data Foundation** or one of the solutions. To try the model before you connect anything, tick **Start with sample data**. To reuse a model you or a colleague saved, choose **From a template file**. See [Save as My Template](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#save-as-my-template). You can also add Data Foundation to a project you already have, or start from **Connect your finance system** when you first sign in. See [Get Data Foundation](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#get-data-foundation).

2. **Connect your system.** On **Summary**, click **Add your finance system**. Under **Add a system** on **Systems**, choose your system, then pick its connection and click **Add system**, or, for QuickBooks Online and Xero, click **Connect to** and approve the access. To bring in another system or another instance, add it the same way; they all feed one consolidated model. Add a file for data outside your systems. See [Connect Your ERP](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#connect-your-erp) and [Systems](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#systems).

3. **Load your data.** A system added with its connection starts loading straight away; otherwise click **Load** on **Summary**. It conforms every source into the model, translates it into your reporting currency and checks it. See [Load Your Data](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#load-your-data).

4. **Answer the questions the load raises.** When a question waits, the next step on **Summary** opens **Questions**, for example to ask whether a second system uses the same account codes as the first, or from which month a system counts for an entity that moved. A question about a revenue class or a team's function is never the next step, as it holds nothing back. Each question comes with Weave's suggested answer, and **Accept all suggested** takes every suggestion at once. Answers apply at the next **Load** or **Apply changes**. See [Map Codes Across Sources](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#map-codes-across-sources), [Choose Which Source Counts From When](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#choose-which-source-counts-from-when) and [Resolve Open Questions With Ask](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#resolve-open-questions-with-ask).

5. **Check that totals tie out.** **Can the numbers be trusted?** on **Summary** shows up to five assurances, and a red one offers **Ask Weave why**. A figure you doubt can be opened with **Explain this number**, which splits net income into lines, entities and accounts and lists its largest documents. See [Data Foundation: Can the Numbers Be Trusted?](/guides/projects/data-foundation-proof/) and [Explain a Number](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#explain-a-number).

6. **Review your hierarchies.** In **Actions ▾** on **Summary**, choose **Hierarchies…** and check the Entity, Account and Department hierarchies. See [Hierarchies](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#hierarchies).

7. **Name things the way your business does.** Give categories, P&L lines, metrics and columns the names your business uses. Reports, metrics and the assistant show them. See [Business Names](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#business-names). In a project with a solution, if the model's cost spreading doesn't match how you work, change it in **Allocations** in the rail. See [Allocation Rules](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#allocation-rules).

8. **Keep it current.** Once a load succeeds, choose **Refresh nightly** in **Actions ▾** on **Summary**. Optionally set up a [Board Pack](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#board-pack), which writes an Excel workbook of the statements and a PDF of the **CFM-Finance - Statements** dashboard to a folder you choose with every load.

9. **Share, document and hand over.** Share the project with your team, choose **Download evidence pack** in **Actions ▾** on **Summary** for a record of what was loaded and how it ties out, and, if you set the project up for a client, make their owner an Architect and then remove yourself. See [Hand the Project Over](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#hand-the-project-over).

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Before a large change to sources or mappings, you don't need to copy anything first: a restore point is made automatically, and **Restore…** in **Actions ▾** on **Summary** puts the project back. See [Restore Points](/guides/projects/data-foundation/#restore-points).
</Aside>

## What You Have at the End

- A Common Finance Model with one chart of accounts, one set of entities and one reporting currency, and a management group profit and loss with intercompany eliminated.
- A model, metrics and an analysis path your team and the assistant can ask questions of.
- A nightly refresh that also makes your board pack, if you set one up.
- An evidence pack and a project your client's owner administers.
