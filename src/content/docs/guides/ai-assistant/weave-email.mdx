## Description

Weave can email you, so you hear about a problem without keeping PlaidCloud
open. There are two kinds of email, and you turn each one on for yourself:

- **An alert** when a workflow run ends and Weave finds a serious problem for
  you to look at: a failed run, or a workflow, step or schedule set up in a way
  that can't work. You get it for the problems Weave would tell you about in
  PlaidCloud, usually because you ran the workflow last or someone assigned the
  problem to you.
- **A daily summary** of the problems Weave told you about that you haven't
  read yet, sent at the time you choose.

Both are off until you turn them on.

## Turn Weave Email On

1. Click the **Weave** button in the Weave dock's header.
2. Choose **Weave email…**.
3. Under **Your email**, tick either or both:
   - **Email me when Weave finds a serious problem for me to look at**
   - **Email me a daily summary of problems Weave told me about that I haven't read**,
     and choose its **Time**.
4. Click **Save**.

Times are in your own time zone, the one your browser uses when you click
**Save**. The window shows it beside **Time**. If you turn the daily summary on
after that time of day, the first one comes within the hour.

If PlaidCloud can't be reached when you click **Save**, the window says *Your
email choices could not be saved. Try again in a moment.* and stays open.

To stop the emails, clear the boxes and click **Save**.

## What an Email Says

Each email names the project, and each problem's workflow, step, table or metric
together with the kind of problem, for example
*"Month End" (workflow) — Last run failed*. It links to the project's
**Weave** tab, where you can read the full finding.

An email never includes figures or values from your data. The sentence Weave
shows in PlaidCloud can quote them, so the email leaves it out.

Weave checks what you can see before it sends anything. Each email is built
from Weave's checks run as you, so it never names something your access hides,
and you get nothing about a project you can no longer open.

The daily summary lists a problem until you read it on the **Weave** tab or the
problem goes away, so a problem you were alerted about yesterday and haven't
read is in today's summary too. With nothing unread, no summary is sent.

## For Administrators

Workspace administrators see a **Workspace** section in the same window.

- **Allow Weave to email members of this workspace** is on until you clear it.
  With it off, Weave sends no email to anyone, and each member's window says
  *Your workspace administrator has turned off Weave email.*
- **Quiet hours**, **From** one hour **Until** another, hold alerts back.
  Quiet hours apply in each member's own time zone, and can run past midnight,
  for example from 20:00 until 07:00. An alert found during quiet hours is sent
  when they end, if the problem is still there and the member hasn't read it.
  Quiet hours don't affect the daily summary, which goes at the time each member
  chose.

Each change is recorded in the workspace's Security Log, with who made it and
when, for example *Turned off Weave email.*

<Aside type="note">
Weave sends email to the address on each member's PlaidCloud account. A member
whose account is deactivated gets no email.
</Aside>
