## Description

An answer from Weave doesn't have to end with the conversation. Under an answer in the
[Weave dock](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-dock/), **Keep ▾** lists the ways to keep it. Each
one prepares a card or a window for you to confirm. Nothing is kept until you do.

| Keep ▾ | What you get | Offered |
|---|---|---|
| **As a standing request** | A report Weave runs every week and sends you. See [Standing Requests](#standing-requests). | Under an answer that read a metric. |
| **As a metric** | A new metric with the answer's filters, which anyone in the project can ask for. See [Keep an Answer as a Metric](#keep-an-answer-as-a-metric). | To project Architects, under a metric answer with a filter. |
| **As a scenario** | A saved what-if. See [Keep an Estimate as a Scenario](#keep-an-estimate-as-a-scenario). | Under a what-if estimate, to members who can edit the project. |
| **As a recipe** | The question, saved for your team to run again. See [Team Recipes](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-team/#team-recipes). | Under any answer. |
| **As a pinned answer** | The answer, shared with the project and re-run for each reader. See [Pinned Answers](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-team/#pinned-answers). | Under an answer Weave can re-run for someone else. |

**Keep ▾** is offered under an answer for 30 days after Weave gives it.

## Standing Requests

A standing request is a report or an alert that Weave runs for you, on a schedule or
after a workflow runs, and sends you the result. Ask for one in the dock in your own words:

- *Every Monday send me margin by region.*
- *After Month-End Close runs, tell me which carriers' freight moved more than 10%.*
- *Tell me when freight passes 2,000,000 this month.*

Weave answers with a card that says what it will run and when, such as *After Month-End
Close runs: Freight by Carrier for the month just closed, against the month before. Weave
tells you which carriers moved more than 10%. First check: after the next run of Month-End
Close.* Click **Save** to start it, **Change…** to ask again, or **Not now**.

**Keep ▾ → As a standing request** under a metric answer prepares the same card in one
step: a weekly report of that answer, on Mondays at 08:00 in your Weave time zone. A period
you asked about, such as this month, moves on with each report.

### Reports and Alerts

- A **report** sends its result every time it runs. A scheduled report whose figures
  haven't changed says *No change since* and the day.
- An **alert** needs a condition: which figure, and what it has to pass, fall below or
  move by. It tells you only when the condition is met. An alert that runs after a
  workflow also checks when its tables are written outside a run.
- A report or alert that runs after a workflow does nothing when the tables it reads
  haven't changed.

Results read like *2 carriers moved more than 10%: Atlas +14.2%, Summit −11.0%. 9
others moved less.*

### How You're Told

When a result is ready, Weave tells you in PlaidCloud, in the dock's jobs, and on your
phone if you use the mobile app, such as *Weave has your Freight by Carrier report. Open the
figures in PlaidCloud.* If you have [Weave email](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-email/) on and
your workspace allows it, you also get an email titled *Weave:* and the request's name, with
a link to open it. Emails never contain figures. During your workspace's quiet hours, the
email isn't sent and the result waits for you in PlaidCloud.

If a request can't run, Weave tells you once, such as *Weave couldn't run your request
Freight by Carrier. Open Requests to see why.*

### Your Requests Run as You

A standing request runs as the person who saved it, with that person's access. If you can
no longer open the project's figures, the request stops and says so. Requests are
personal: only you see, run, change, pause or remove yours.

## The Requests Page

Open the project's **Weave** tab and click **Requests** in the **Monitor** rail. The page
lists *Reports, alerts and recipes you asked Weave to run, on a schedule or after a workflow
runs.*

Each request shows its name and its state (**Working**, **Needs you** or **Paused**), Weave's
sentence about what it runs, when it runs, with the time zone, and its last result, or **No
result yet**.

- **Run now** runs it straight away.
- **Pause** stops it running until you click **Resume**.
- **⋯ → Change** opens the dock with your request filled in, for you to say what to change.
- **⋯ → Remove** stops it for good, after you confirm.

Click **New request** to open the dock and ask for one.

## Keep an Answer as a Metric

When Weave answers from a metric with a filter, such as revenue for the Retail channel, a
project Architect can keep that filtered figure as a metric of its own.

1. Under the answer, choose **Keep ▾ → As a metric**.
2. In the **New metric** window, check the **Name**, such as *Revenue — Retail*, and the
   **Definition**: the measure and the filters.
3. Click **Save**.

The metric is live as soon as it's saved: anyone in the project who can ask for metrics can
ask Weave for it by name. It's saved without the period you asked about, so it can be asked
for any period. To stop Weave answering from it, click **Retire**.

- A name that's already taken gets a number, such as *Revenue — Retail (2)*.
- An answer with no filter other than a period is the metric itself, so there's nothing new
  to keep. Weave says so.
- A metric built from other metrics can't be kept with a filter.

**Open the dashboard editor** in the window opens Dashboards in a new tab, for charting the
new metric.

## Keep an Estimate as a Scenario

When you ask Weave a what-if about an allocation's output, such as *what if freight rises
8%*, it answers with an estimate. See
[Ask a What-If](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-numbers/#ask-a-what-if).

To keep it, choose **Keep ▾ → As a scenario**. The **What if** window opens with the change
filled in and a name such as *Freight +8%*. Click **Save…**, then **Save**, to keep it.
Closing the window without saving discards it.

Saved scenarios change nothing in the project, and everyone in the project sees them. A
saved scenario is worked out again on the current data each time it's opened. A project
keeps up to 100 saved scenarios. See
[Try a Driver What-If](/guides/allocations/results/driver-what-if/).
