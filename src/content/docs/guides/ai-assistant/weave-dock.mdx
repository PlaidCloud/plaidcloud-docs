## Description

The **Weave dock** is Weave's panel on the right-hand edge of PlaidCloud. It is there beside
every app and every window in it, so you can ask Weave about what you're looking at without
leaving it. The same dock holds your Weave conversations, the changes Weave proposes, and the
jobs it is working on for you.

Weave does nothing until you ask. Opening a project or the dock doesn't scan anything or call
an AI model.

## Open and Close the Dock

Closed, the dock is a narrow strip with the Weave mark. A number on the strip counts what is
waiting for you, and a moving dot shows that Weave is answering or one of your jobs is working.

- Click the strip, or press **Ctrl+J** (**Cmd+J** on a Mac), to open the dock. Click **›** or
  press the same keys to fold it away.
- Drag the dock's left edge to set its width, from 300 pixels up to half the window.
- PlaidCloud remembers whether the dock is open and how wide it is, for you, in whichever
  browser you sign in from.

When the dock is open, its header shows the conversation in front, with its type when it isn't
an ordinary one (**Fix**, **Help** or **Weave job**), what Weave is looking at, **Conversations**,
which lists your past conversations, and **New conversation**.

If your workspace administrator has turned Weave chat off, the box says so. Fixing a step still
works when the fix assistant is on, and the answers that need no AI model always work.

## What Weave Is Looking At

Weave starts from what you have open: the step, workflow or table you are in, the rows or steps
you selected, or the error you clicked. Each shows as a chip above the box you type in, such as
**step Allocate by Driver** or **3 selected steps**. Click **✕** on a chip to leave it out of your
next question.

If Weave can't open something you had open, it says so and doesn't use it.

## Ask Weave from a Right-Click

Right-click an object, or open its **⋯** menu, to see a **Weave** section at the top of the menu.
Only the actions that apply to that object, and that your role allows, are listed. For example,
**Check** and **Where is this used** need permission to read the project.

| Right-click | Weave actions |
|---|---|
| A step on the canvas or in the step list | **Explain**, **Where is this used**, **What changed**, **Fix**, **Change…**, **Document**, **Ask Weave** |
| Several selected steps | **Explain**, **Ask Weave** |
| A workflow in the list | **Explain**, **What changed**, **Check**, **Change…**, **Document**, **Ask Weave** |
| A table in the list or in Table Explorer | **Explain**, **Where is this used**, **Change…**, **Document**, **Ask Weave** |
| A column in Table Explorer | **Explain**, **Ask Weave** |
| A dashboard in the Dashboards list | **Explain**, **Tables it reads**, **Ask Weave** |
| Cells or selected rows in Table Explorer | **Ask Weave** |
| An error line in the log | **Explain this error**, **Fix**, **Ask Weave** |
| A warning line in the log | **Explain**, **Ask Weave** |

**Fix** is offered on a step when its last run failed or a finding in the project's
[Monitor](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-tab/#monitor) offers one, and on error lines in the log but
not warning lines. **Change…** is offered only if your role can edit that object. Findings in Monitor offer the same actions: **Fix**
or **Ask Weave** is the button on the row, and the rest are under **⋯**.

- **Explain**, **Where is this used**, **What changed**, **Check** and **Tables it reads** answer
  at once in a small window beside what you clicked. They read what they need from your project
  and most don't call an AI model. Each answer ends with **How Weave knows**, which says where it
  came from.
- **Fix** and **Change…** open the dock and prepare a change for you to approve. **Change…** starts
  the box with *Change this step so*, ready for you to finish the sentence.
- **Document** and **Ask Weave** open the dock with what you clicked already in front of Weave.

You can also type `/` in the box to pick from the Weave actions for what is in front of you.
Under an empty box, Weave suggests up to four things to ask. In a project with nothing open, one
of them is **What needs attention?**.

## Follow Weave's Progress

When you press Send, a short list above the answer shows what Weave is doing, in plain words,
starting with **Reading what you have open**. Each line gets a check mark when it finishes, and
the list folds away when the answer is complete.

## Approve a Change

Weave never changes anything until you approve it. When it proposes a change, it shows a card
with what will change, how far it reaches, and what happens next. The change is saved only when
you approve the card in the browser: you can't approve a card from the mobile app, and Weave
can't approve one for you.

A card shows:

- What changes, in a box for each setting titled with the step and the setting, with the old
  value and the new one on separate lines. A SQL statement, expression or long mapping is shown
  whole, with its line breaks.
- What Weave will do after saving, as a plain sentence such as *Then: re-run Allocate by Driver
  with ERP posting off and check…*. Weave turns ERP posting off for every run a card starts.
- How far the change reaches, when it affects steps in other workflows, such as *Changes to this
  step affect 8 downstream steps in 2 workflows.* A step or workflow you can't open is counted,
  not named.
- Anything Weave couldn't include, such as a step your role can't edit, as *Not added:* and the
  reason.
- Whether the change can be undone. A card that can't be undone says **This can't be undone**
  before you approve it.

The card's buttons are **Approve** followed by the number of changes, such as **Approve 1 change**
or **Approve 6 changes**, **Not now** to leave things as they are, and **Change…** to ask Weave
again with your request filled in. **Change…** never edits the card: Weave answers with a new one.

One press covers the whole card, even when it changes several steps. After you approve, the card
shows that the change is applied and Weave re-runs what it fixed and says plainly whether the
problem is gone, for example *Fixed and re-run: Allocate by Driver succeeded in 4 min, 46,812
rows, within its usual range.* If Weave couldn't confirm, it says why, for example that the
workflow was already running, and never says the problem is fixed. A step that posts to an ERP
system or runs another workflow isn't re-run.

For fixing a failed step, see [Fixing Workflow Errors with AI](/guides/ai-assistant/fixing-workflow-errors/).

### Cards Waiting for You

When Weave prepares a change while you're away, because a job you started needed your decision,
the card waits in that conversation until you decide. In that conversation, a row above the chat
shows a **Waiting for you** badge, the card's title and an **Open** button. Choose **Open** to see
the card with its approval button, labeled *Weave prepared this while you were away.* Only you can
see and decide it, and only on the web.

If the step or object changed since Weave prepared the card, approving it first shows how things
look now and asks again. Nothing is saved until you approve what you can see. A card is closed
with the reason when what it would change no longer exists.

## Undo a Change

Every change Weave makes that can be undone shows **Undo**, with one line on what it covers. On a
card that fixed several steps, one **Undo** covers all of them. The same button is on the change's
entry in [Monitor → Activity](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-activity/#undo-a-step-fix), so you can
undo after the conversation is closed.

**Undo** opens a window showing what undoing does: the settings it puts back, each as it is
**Now** and **After Undo**, or what it removes and what it keeps, with the reason. Choose **Undo**
to go ahead, **Undo & Run** to put a step's settings back and run it, or **Cancel**.

Undo restores only what Weave changed. Edits other people made since are kept, and anything Undo
can't restore is listed. A project Weave created is moved to the Projects trash, where you can
restore it.

## Jobs That Keep Working

Some things Weave does take a while. A job keeps working if you close the dock, change apps or
close the browser tab.

- Choose **Weave jobs…** from the **Weave** button in the header to see your jobs. Each shows its
  title, its state (**Working**, with how long, **Needs you**, **Done** or **Stopped**) and its
  latest progress line. **Open** takes you to the conversation at its answer or its card, and
  **Stop** ends a job that is working.
- The **Weave** button shows a marker while any job is working.
- When a job ends, or needs a decision from you, a message appears in whichever PlaidCloud window
  you have open. If the job ran for more than a minute and wasn't stopped, you also get a
  notification on your phone if you use the mobile app. Opening it takes you to the job's
  conversation. Decisions are made on the web.

## Open a Link to Weave

A link to PlaidCloud can open the dock with its context ready and an action waiting as a button,
such as **Fix**. Weave runs by itself only an action that changes nothing.
A link never starts a run.

## Weave in the Monitor Tab

The project's **Weave** tab keeps **Monitor**. Its chats and conversation history are in the dock.
Someone whose role has no **Findings** sees one line there, *Ask Weave in the panel on the right*,
with **Open Weave**.
