This page is written for Weave's users and for your security and privacy teams.
The same information is in PlaidCloud: choose **How Weave uses your data…** in the
**Weave** menu in the Weave dock's header, or **How Weave uses your data** below any AI chat or,
for workspace administrators, in **AI Features**.

## Who Answers Weave

Weave chat, the fix assistant and the other features listed under
[AI Features](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-ai-features/) are answered by a
third-party AI provider that PlaidCloud chooses, calls and pays for. This
includes Weave in the [PlaidCloud mobile app](/guides/mobile-app/weave-on-mobile/).

PlaidCloud may change the provider, or send different requests to different
models, at any time for quality, cost or performance. Whichever provider
answers, its terms with PlaidCloud decide what it may do with your data. See
[How Your Data Is Handled](#how-your-data-is-handled).

## What Weave Sends

To answer you, Weave sends your message and whatever it reads to answer it. That
can include:

- the names and definitions of projects, tables, steps and workflows
- run logs and error messages
- rows from tables it queries, at most 500 from any one query
- members of dimensions it reads, which can be every member of a dimension
- the names of people Weave reads about, such as who wrote a comment or a member you have selected
- the name of anything else you have selected, such as a document or a folder

Weave reads only what you can read yourself.

When you speak to Weave, your browser turns your speech into text. Where it can't do that on
your device, it may send the audio to its own speech service. PlaidCloud never receives the
audio, only the text of your question.

## How Your Data Is Handled

PlaidCloud's terms with each AI provider decide whether the provider may keep
the data Weave sends, for how long, and whether it may use it to train AI
models. Those terms can differ from one provider to another.

## Your Conversations

PlaidCloud stores your Weave conversations so you can come back to them. Only
you can open them in PlaidCloud.

## Your Own AI Connection and AI Tools

AI steps in your workflows, and assistants that use your workspace's own AI
connection, send data to the provider you chose for that connection, under your
agreement with them. See
[Using Your Own Controlled LLM](/integrations/ai-architecture/private-llm/).

The same applies when you use PlaidCloud from your own AI tools, such as Claude
or Microsoft Copilot. PlaidCloud's own AI provider is not used for these.

## Turning AI Features Off

Workspace administrators can turn off each AI feature PlaidCloud provides, in
[AI Features](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-ai-features/).
