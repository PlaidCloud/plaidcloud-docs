This page is written for Weave's users and for your security and privacy teams.
The same information is in PlaidCloud: choose **How Weave uses your data…** in the
**Weave** menu in the header, or **How Weave uses your data** below any AI chat or,
for workspace administrators, in **AI Features**.

## Most of Weave Uses No AI

Weave's checks and findings are worked out from your project's own records
without AI. A finding goes to the AI provider only through an AI feature, such as
**Fix** or the summary above a workflow's **Check**.

## Which AI Provider Answers Weave

Weave chat, the fix assistant and the other features listed under
[AI Features](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-ai-features/) are answered by an AI
provider that PlaidCloud calls and pays for. This includes Weave in the
[PlaidCloud mobile app](/guides/mobile-app/weave-on-mobile/).

Each workspace uses one of these:

- **Google Gemini on Vertex AI**
- **xAI's Grok**

**How Weave uses your data** in PlaidCloud names the provider and region your
workspace uses.

## What Weave Sends

To answer you, Weave sends your message and whatever it reads to answer it. That
can include:

- the names and definitions of projects, tables, steps and workflows
- run logs and error messages
- rows from tables it queries, at most 500 from any one query

Weave reads only what you can read yourself.

## Training and Retention

| Provider | Trains on your data | How long requests are kept |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Gemini on Vertex AI** | No. Google does not use this data to train its models. | Confirmation pending. |
| **xAI's Grok** | As xAI's standard terms allow. | As xAI's standard terms allow. |

PlaidCloud has no zero-data-retention agreement with xAI. Workspaces are moving
to Google Gemini on Vertex AI.

## Your Conversations

PlaidCloud stores your Weave conversations so you can come back to them. Only
you can open them in PlaidCloud.

## Your Own AI Connection and AI Tools

AI steps in your workflows, and assistants that use your workspace's own AI
connection, send data to the provider you chose for that connection, under your
agreement with them. See
[Using Your Own Controlled LLM](/integrations/ai-architecture/private-llm/).

The same applies when you use PlaidCloud from your own AI tools, such as Claude
or Microsoft Copilot. PlaidCloud's own AI provider is not used for these.

## Turning AI Features Off

Workspace administrators can turn off each AI feature PlaidCloud provides, in
[AI Features](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-ai-features/).
