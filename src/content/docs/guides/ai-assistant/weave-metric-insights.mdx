## Description

Weave reads each of your project's [approved metrics](/integrations/ai-coding-agents/metric-definitions/)
once a day and looks at its latest finished period. When the metric's total for
that period is outside its usual range, and one item in a breakdown, such as one
customer, one product or one plant, changed far more than its usual share of the
total would suggest, Weave reports it on the project's
[Weave tab](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-tab/) and names the item.

No AI model is involved. The check is a fixed rule, and every sentence is written
in advance from the figures Weave read.

## What a Finding Says

```
Weave noticed the metric "Revenue": it came to 16.4M in April 2026, against an
average of 11.2M (usual range 8.9M to 13.5M) over the 12 months before.
"Seasonal Specials" (by product_group), usually 2% of the total, rose from an
average of 210.0K to 1.7M, 1.5M of the rise of 5.2M (29%); the other 3.7M came
from elsewhere
```

Amounts are shortened to K, M and B, and no currency or unit is shown, because a
metric definition doesn't record one. A metric with a date column on a calendar
year names the month, such as *April 2026*; any other names the period, such as
*period 8 of fiscal 2025*.

When one item moved by more than the whole total did, the finding says so and
says that everything else moved the other way. An item with nothing in the earlier
periods is described as having had none.

**How Weave knows** shows the period, the total, the average of the earlier
periods, how many periods were compared, the usual range, the item that moved
most and any others that also moved far more than usual.

## Which Metrics Weave Checks

A metric is checked when it is **approved**, not retired, and:

- it has a **date column**, or a **year and period column**, so it has periods
  to compare;
- it is a **sum or a count**, which Weave can split into what each item added;
- it has at least one **breakdown column**, such as customer or plant, to show
  what moved; and
- it is **not calculated from other metrics**.

Weave reads the approved version only, never a draft, and reads it as the
Architect who approved it, seeing only what they can see. Weave checks up to 50
approved metrics in each project, the earliest approved first; any beyond those
are listed as not checked.

A metric that doesn't qualify, or that Weave couldn't read, is listed as one
**Weave hasn't checked**, with the reason. It is never counted as fine.

## How the Check Works

**The period judged.** For a metric with a date column, that is the latest
period that ended at least 7 days ago, so a period still being loaded during your
close isn't judged. For a metric with year and period columns, which carry no
dates, it is the period before the latest one with figures.

**The usual range.** Weave compares the period's total with up to 12 periods
before it and needs at least 6 of them with figures. The range comes from how
much those periods varied.

**Seasonal periods.** A total outside the range is not reported when the same
period in at least two of the three years before came out similarly. On a
calendar whose periods divide into quarters, it is also not reported when it is
in line with the same position in earlier quarters, such as a five-week month.

**The item that moved.** Weave splits the total by each breakdown column,
starting with those tied to a dimension, and names an item only when its share of
the change is far larger than its usual share of the total. A change spread
across many items, each moving in proportion, is not reported.

**Late adjustments.** Once a problem is reported for a period, its figures stay
as first read while later readings stay within half a percent of them, so a
small late adjustment doesn't report it again as new.

## What Weave Doesn't Check

- A total outside its usual range, when no one item stands out.
- One item's share shifting while the total stays in its usual range.
- Whether the move is a mistake or real business.
- Drafts of the metric.
- Earlier periods, which are used only for comparison.

## Who Sees It and Who Is Told

The finding belongs to the person who **approved** the metric's current
version, and they are told in PlaidCloud between runs. Like any finding, it also
counts toward the [daily digest](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-findings/#a-digest)
of anyone who has turned the digest on. If the approver is no longer an
Architect of the project, Weave stops checking the metric until a current
Architect approves it.

Anyone who can read the project sees the finding on the Weave tab. In a project
with [row access](/administration/access/managing-security-groups-and-assignments/),
only Architects get these findings, because the figures were read with the
approver's access.

Each finding is recorded in the project's
[Weave activity history](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-activity/) when it is first
reported. When a later reading of the same period no longer shows the problem,
the history says Weave no longer found it. When the next period closes, it says
Weave's daily check moved on to that period. When the metric is retired or no
longer approved, it says Weave stopped checking it.

## Go To Rows

**Go To Rows** on a finding opens Table Explorer on the metric's source table,
filtered to the item, the period and the metric's own filters, so you can see the
rows behind the change. It appears for people who can read tables, and not for a
metric that uses a lookup table, whose rows the source table alone can't show.

<Aside type="note">
A newly approved metric is read within a day. Until then it is listed as one
Weave hasn't read yet.
</Aside>

## When Weave Hasn't Checked a Metric

| Reason shown | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| It is calculated from other metrics | Nothing; Weave checks only base metrics. |
| It is an average, a minimum, a maximum or a count of distinct values | Nothing; only sums and counts can be split by item. |
| It has no breakdown | Add a breakdown column, such as customer, and approve the new version. |
| It has no date or period column | Add a time binding and approve the new version. |
| Its year or period column holds values such as *2024-01* | Point the time binding at whole-number year and period columns, or at a date column. |
| It has only a few periods of figures | Wait: Weave needs 6 earlier periods. |
| It has no figures for the period | Check that the period's data loaded. |
| The approver is no longer an Architect | Have a current Architect approve it. |
| Reading it took more than 2 minutes | Weave tries again the next day. |
| Its breakdowns have more than 50,000 figures | Weave doesn't check it while that is so. |
| The project has more than 50 approved metrics | Retire metrics nobody uses; the earliest approved are checked first. |
| Weave could not read its approved version | Weave tries again the next day. |
| Weave's daily check last ran some days ago | Ask your PlaidCloud administrator. |
