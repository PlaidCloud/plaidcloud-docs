## Description

Weave reads each of your project's [metrics](/integrations/ai-coding-agents/metric-definitions/)
once a day and looks at its latest finished period. When the metric's total for
that period is outside its usual range, and one item in a breakdown, such as one
customer, one product or one plant, changed far more than its usual share of the
total would suggest, Weave reports it on the project's
[Weave tab](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-tab/) and names the item.

## What a Finding Says

```
Weave noticed the metric "Revenue": it came to 16.4M in April 2026, against an
average of 11.2M (usual range 8.9M to 13.5M) over the 12 months before.
"Seasonal Specials" (by product_group), usually 2% of the total, rose from an
average of 210.0K to 1.7M, 1.5M of the rise of 5.2M (29%); the other 3.7M came
from elsewhere
```

Amounts are shortened to K, M and B, and no currency or unit is shown, because a
metric definition doesn't record one. A metric with a date column on a calendar
year names the month, such as *April 2026*; any other names the period, such as
*period 8 of fiscal 2025*.

When one item moved by more than the whole total did, the finding says so and
says that everything else moved the other way. An item with nothing in the earlier
periods is described as having had none.

**How Weave knows** shows the period, the total, the average of the earlier
periods, how many periods were compared, the usual range, the item that moved
most and any others that also moved far more than usual.

## Which Metrics Weave Checks

A metric is checked when it is not retired and:

- it has a **date column**, or a **year and period column**, so it has periods
  to compare;
- it is a **sum or a count**, which Weave can split into what each item added;
- it has at least one **breakdown column**, such as customer or plant, to show
  what moved; and
- it is **not calculated from other metrics**.

Weave reads each metric's current version as the Architect who last saved it,
seeing only what they can see. Weave checks up to 50 metrics in each project,
the earliest saved first; any beyond those are listed as not checked.

A metric that doesn't qualify, or that Weave couldn't read, is listed as one
**Weave hasn't checked**, with the reason. It is never counted as fine.

## How the Check Works

**The period judged.** For a metric with a date column, that is the latest
period that ended at least 7 days ago, so a period still being loaded during your
close isn't judged. For a metric with year and period columns, which carry no
dates, it is the period before the latest regular period with figures, or the
year's last period once an adjustment period after it, such as period 13 to 16
of a 12-period year, has figures. Period 0 and adjustment periods are never judged.

**The usual range.** Weave compares the period's total with up to 12 periods
before it and needs at least 6 of them with figures. The range comes from how
much those periods varied.

**Seasonal periods.** A total outside the range is not reported when the same
period in at least two of the three years before came out similarly. On a
calendar whose periods divide into quarters, it is also not reported when it is
in line with the same position in earlier quarters, such as a five-week month.

**The item that moved.** Weave splits the total by up to five breakdown columns,
starting with those tied to a dimension, and names an item only when its share of
the change is far larger than its usual share of the total. The finding leads
with the item that moved most by amount. A change spread
across many items, each moving in proportion, is not reported.

**Late adjustments.** Once a problem is reported for a period, its figures stay
as first read while later readings stay within half a percent of them, so a
small late adjustment doesn't report it again as new.

## Items That Started or Stopped

Each day Weave also splits each metric by its breakdown columns and reports an
item that **started** or **stopped** adding to it, such as a new plant or a
customer whose orders ended. Each item is its own finding, reported once, when
the change has held for 3 periods.

```
Weave noticed the metric "Net Revenue": "Plant 12" (by plant) first had figures
in September 2024, after nothing in any of the 8 months on record before it. In
the 3 months to November 2024 it added an average of 4.1M a month, 3.2% of the
metric's total in those months.
```

```
Weave noticed the metric "Net Revenue": "Plant 4" (by plant) has had nothing
above 0.1% of the metric's total in any of the 3 months after November 2026, to
February 2027. In the 12 months on record to November 2026 it added an average of 3.1M a
month, 2.9% of the metric's total in those months.
```

- **Started.** The item had nothing above 0.1% of the total in every earlier
  period, with at least 6 on record, and has had at least 2% of the total in each
  of the last 3. A start is reported only within the item's first 12 periods on
  record, and the finding is dated to its first period above 0.1%, even when the
  item grew slowly before it reached 2%.
- **Stopped.** The item had more than 0.1% of the total in each of its last 12
  periods on record before it stopped, or in every one if there are fewer, at least
  6, with at least 2% of the total in at least half of them, and has had nothing
  above 0.1% in each of the 3 periods since. The finding is dated to its last
  period above 0.1%.

An item's share is of every item's figures taken together, so a cost stored as a
negative, or a credit that nets the total down, doesn't make an item look larger
than it is. A period with no figures at all, such as one before the data begins,
counts neither way, and neither do period 0 and adjustment periods.

**How Weave knows** shows the period judged and the item, with when it started or
stopped. **Go To Metric** shows the item in the period it started, or in its last
period above 0.1%.

### What This Check Doesn't Report

- An item with more than 0.1% of the total in any earlier period, as starting,
  however long the gap since; or one with 0.1% or less in any of its last 12
  periods on record before it stopped, such as a seasonal item, as stopping.
- An item smaller than 2% of the total.
- A breakdown with one item at a time holding the whole total, such as fiscal
  quarter or fiscal year. Weave skips such a breakdown in both checks, but it still
  counts toward the five breakdown columns Weave reads.
- Whether the latest periods are fully posted: a late load or an unfinished close
  can look like an item stopping.

## What Weave Doesn't Check

- A total outside its usual range, when no one item stands out.
- One item's share shifting while the total stays in its usual range.
- Breakdown columns beyond the first five, or one with more than 50,000 figures
  across its items and periods.
- A breakdown with one item at a time holding the whole total, such as fiscal
  quarter or fiscal year.
- Whether the move is a mistake or real business.
- Earlier periods, which are used only for comparison.

## Who Sees It and Who Is Told

The finding belongs to the person who **last saved** the metric, and they are told in PlaidCloud between runs. Like any finding, it also
counts toward the [daily digest](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-findings/#a-digest)
of anyone who has turned the digest on. If the person who last saved it is no longer an
Architect of the project, Weave stops checking the metric until a current
Architect saves it.

Anyone who can read the project sees the finding on the Weave tab. In a project
with [row access](/administration/access/managing-security-groups-and-assignments/),
only Architects get these findings, because the figures were read with the
saver's access.

Each finding is recorded in the project's
[Weave activity history](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-activity/) when it is first
reported. When a later reading of the same period no longer shows the problem,
the history says Weave no longer found it. When the next period closes, it says
Weave's daily check moved on to that period. When the metric is retired, it says Weave stopped checking it.

## Go To Metric

**Go To Metric** on a finding opens the metric, calculated when you click it and
under your own row access. For an unusual total, it shows the item Weave named in
the period it judged. For a metric Weave couldn't check, including every metric
calculated from other metrics, it shows the metric's total. A ✓ names the version
of the metric the figures came from. They are the metric as it stands now, so if
it has changed since Weave read it, they can differ from the finding's.

Under the figures, **Go To Rows** opens Table Explorer on the metric's source
table, filtered to the item, the period and the metric's own filters, so you can
see the rows behind the change. It appears for people who can read tables, and
not for a metric that uses a lookup table, whose rows the source table alone
can't show.

<Aside type="note">
A newly saved metric is read within a day, unless the project already has 50
metrics. Until then it is listed as one Weave hasn't read yet.
</Aside>

## When Weave Hasn't Checked a Metric

| Reason shown | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| It is calculated from other metrics | Nothing; Weave checks only base metrics. |
| It is an average, a minimum, a maximum or a count of distinct values | Nothing; only sums and counts can be split by item. |
| It has no breakdown | Add a breakdown column, such as customer, and save the new version. |
| It has no date or period column | Add a time binding and save the new version. |
| Its year or period column holds values such as *2024-01* | Point the time binding at whole-number year and period columns, or at a date column. |
| It has only a few periods of figures | Wait: Weave needs 6 earlier periods. |
| It has no figures for the period | Check that the period's data loaded. |
| The person who last saved it is no longer an Architect | Have a current Architect save it. |
| Reading it took more than 2 minutes | Weave tries again the next day. |
| Every one of its breakdowns has more than 50,000 figures | Weave doesn't check it while that is so. |
| Its total is outside its usual range, but a breakdown has more than 50,000 figures | Weave can't tell whether one item in that breakdown drove the change. Look at that breakdown yourself, or remove it from the metric's breakdown columns. |
| Its total is outside its usual range, but each breakdown has one item at a time holding the whole total | Add a breakdown such as customer or plant; a fiscal quarter or year can't show which item moved. |
| It has too few periods of figures to tell whether an item started or stopped | Wait: Weave needs 9 periods, the last 3 without a gap. |
| The project has more than 50 metrics | Retire metrics nobody uses; the earliest saved are checked first. |
| Weave could not read its current version | Weave tries again the next day. |
| Weave's daily check last ran some days ago | Ask your PlaidCloud administrator. |
