## Description

What one person learns from Weave can be used by the whole project. A pinned answer or a
recipe is always worked out again as the person reading or running it, with their own
access, so nobody sees figures their role hides.

## Pinned Answers

Pin an answer to share it with everyone in the project.

1. Under the answer in the [Weave dock](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-dock/), choose
   **Keep ▾ → As a pinned answer**.
2. Weave says the project it pins the answer to, and the title it will be listed under,
   which is your question. Everyone in the project will see your question and how Weave
   answered it.
3. Click **Pin**.

The answer is marked **Pinned**. **Copy link** copies a link to it.

When a teammate opens a pinned answer, Weave re-runs it as them, so the figures are their
own. The answer says who pinned it and when, and that Weave re-ran it for them. If what
they can see differs from what you saw, they're told *You see different data here.*, with
**Ask again**.

When a teammate asks Weave a question that matches a pinned answer, a line above Weave's
answer offers it: *A teammate pinned an answer about this*, with **Show**.

- Weave can pin answers it can re-run for someone else: answers from metrics and value
  searches, and the answers to **Explain**, **Where is this used**, **What changed**,
  **Check**, **Tables it reads**, **Preview**, **Document** and **What needs attention?**.
  Other answers can be kept as a [recipe](#team-recipes) instead, and Weave offers
  **Keep as a recipe**.
- A teammate sees a pinned answer only if they can open everything it's about.
- Whoever pinned the answer, or a project Architect, can **Unpin** it.

## Team Recipes

A recipe is a question saved so anyone on your team can run it again, the way you asked it.

1. Under an answer in the dock, choose **Keep ▾ → As a recipe**.
2. Weave names the recipe from your question and says that everyone in the project, and
   the AI tools they connect to PlaidCloud, will see your question and how Weave answered
   it. Click **Save**, or **Change…** to ask again.

To run a recipe, type `/` in the dock and choose it under **Recipes**, or type `/` and its
short name. Weave runs it as you, in a conversation titled *Recipe:* and the recipe's name.
The parts of a recipe that only read data run without calling an AI model. Any other part is
asked of Weave again. A change a recipe proposes is a card that you approve, as with any
other.

- Anyone who can open everything a recipe works on can see it, run it and set a trigger on
  it. Others don't see it.
- Whoever saved a recipe, or a project Architect, can rename or delete it. Deleting a
  recipe stops the triggers people set on it.
- AI tools connected to PlaidCloud over MCP list the project's recipes too.

### Run a Recipe on a Trigger

To have a recipe run by itself, choose **Run on a trigger…** from its **⋯** menu on the
project's **Overview**, and say when in the dock, such as *every Monday at 08:00* or *after
Month-End Close runs*. The recipe becomes a
[standing request](/guides/ai-assistant/weave-requests/#standing-requests) that runs as you,
listed on the **Requests** page as *Recipe:* and its name. A trigger after a workflow run
does nothing when nothing the recipe reads has changed. If any part of the recipe asks Weave,
the card says it *Uses AI each time it runs.*

**Run on a trigger…** is offered for recipes that read at least one table.

## From Your Team on the Project's Overview

Once something is shared in a project, its **Overview** has a **From your team** card:
*Answers your teammates pinned and recipes they saved, run as you.* Each pinned answer shows
its title, who pinned it and when. Each recipe shows its name and author, with **Run** and a
**⋯** menu with **Run on a trigger…**, **Rename…** and **Delete**.

## Comments and @Weave

Steps, workflows, tables and findings have comment threads. Choose **Comment** from the
**Weave** section of an object's right-click menu, or use the comments button in the
workflow canvas toolbar for the workflow's own thread. On a finding, open it and click
**Comments**.

Type in **Add a comment** and click **Add comment**. Comments can't be edited or removed.

- **Mention a teammate** by name, and they're told in PlaidCloud that you mentioned them. If
  they can't see what the comment is about, they aren't told, and you're told so.
- **Mention @Weave** to ask Weave a question in the thread. A note under the box says that
  everyone who can see the step, workflow, table or finding will see your question and how
  Weave answered it.

Weave answers as you, with your access, in a conversation of yours titled *Comment on* and
what the thread is about. The thread gets one line, such as *Weave answered Dana about
Allocate by Driver.*, with **Open the answer**. You open your own conversation from it, and
anyone else sees the answer re-run as them. If Weave proposes a change, the line says it's
waiting for you to approve it, and only you see **Review the change**.

If Weave can't take the question, because Weave isn't set up in the workspace or you haven't
agreed to how Weave uses your data, your comment is still saved and the thread says why.
