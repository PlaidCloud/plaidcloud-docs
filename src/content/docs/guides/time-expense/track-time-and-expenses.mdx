The **Project** app — sometimes called Time & Expense, or T&E — is where you enter your own time, get it approved, review trips and receipts, run master projects and their teams, and bill customers. It's one docked workspace, not a set of floating windows: a **navigation rail** down the left opens each screen as a **pane** in the centre, and opening a record — a timesheet for approval, a trip, a master project, an invoice — docks its detail in a **drawer** on the right without losing the list you were working from.

## The Workspace Layout

The rail is grouped into three sections:

- **My work** — Home, Time, Approvals, Trips.
- **Manage** — Setup, People, Trip Assignment.
- **Billing** — Invoices, Accounts, Voids.

A count badge appears on a rail item when there's something waiting on it — sheets you haven't submitted, sheets awaiting your approval, unassigned trips, past-due invoices — and clears once there's nothing left. Every pane keeps its place when you switch away and back, and deep links work the same way they do everywhere else in PlaidCloud: the URL follows whichever pane or drawer is in front, so a link to a specific timesheet or invoice opens straight to it.

## Who Can See What

What the rail shows you depends on your role, and a destination you don't have permission for is left off the rail entirely rather than shown and disabled:

- Anyone with access to the app sees **Home**, enters and submits their own time on **Time**, sees their own trips and receipts on **Trips**, and sees themselves — and, where visible, their own approvers and assistants — on **People**.
- **Approvals** and **Trip Assignment** only appear for members set up to approve time or manage trip billing.
- **Billing** — Invoices, Accounts, and Voids — only appears for members with billing permissions on the project.
- Rate, dollar amounts, PO value, and billing rates are visible only to members with billing permissions; everyone else sees hours and status with no money attached.

An administrator grants these through [Security Groups](/administration/access/managing-security-groups-and-assignments/).

## Home

Home is a set of cards, each shown only if it applies to you:

- **Hours this week**, compared with last week.
- **Sheets not submitted** — click through to **Time**, filtered to your drafts.
- **Awaiting your approval** — click through to **Approvals**.
- **Unassigned trips** — click through to **Trip Assignment** (finance).
- **Open invoices** and **Past-due invoices**, each with a count and total — click through to **Invoices**, pre-filtered (finance).

## Time

Time is a weekly grid, one row per project, work location, and billable flag, with a column for each day, Sunday through Saturday, and a **Total** column and footer row. Choosing a project shows only the work locations set up for it. Move week with **Previous week**, **This week**, and **Next week**; add a row with **Add row**. Saving your first row for a week creates that week's sheet — an empty week has no sheet yet.

Each sheet carries a status chip — **Draft**, **Submitted**, **Approved** — and a separate billing badge, **Partly billed** or **Billed**, that can appear at any status once time has been invoiced. An approved sheet shows a banner explaining it's locked and disables every control; an older sheet for a week that already has a newer one shows read-only with a note and a **Show newest** link. Add a note in the **Memo** field, then **Submit** when the week is ready for review. **Delete sheet** removes a draft entirely, with a confirmation first. A side panel labeled **My sheets** lists your sheets, filterable to **All / Draft / Submitted / Approved**, so you can jump back to an earlier week.

With billing permissions, the grid also shows **Rate**, **Amount**, and **Billed** per row; without them, only hours and status appear.

## Approvals

Approvals lists every submitted sheet waiting on you, oldest first. Select one to open it in the drawer: the member, the week, the memo, and every entry with its daily hours and total. **Approve** moves it off the queue and the pane selects the next sheet for you. You can never approve your own sheet, even if a self-approver relationship exists on the account — that's enforced on every approval path, not just the obvious one. With billing permissions, the drawer also shows rate and amount per entry.

## Trips and Trip Assignment

**Trips** shows your own trips, sourced from Ramp, read-only — filter by **All**, **Not Submitted**, **Not Approved**, **Approved**, or **Quick** (your ten most recent). Open a trip to see its expenses and receipts in the drawer, preview a receipt, or download **Receipts PDF** — a single PDF merging every receipt on that trip. With billing permissions the list also shows each trip's billable total.

**Trip Assignment** is where finance assigns trips to projects, across three tabs — **My Trips**, **My Team**, **All Unassigned** — with **Assign to Project**, **Mark Billable**, and **Mark Non-Billable** available on a selection.

## Setup

Setup is where master projects, their sub-projects, billing roles, team, and work locations live. The list opens to your master projects — filter **Open / Closed / All**, tick **Mine**, or search — with **New Master Project** to create one. Opening a master shows its header (customer, PO number, and, with billing permissions, PO value) with **Edit** and **Archive**, over tabs:

- **Projects** — the master's sub-projects; each carries its own time/expense-enabled flags, dates, status, and project manager.
- **Team** — who's assigned to a project, their role, admin/inactive flags, time and expense limits, and hours used against those limits; billing permissions add rate, amount, and billable.
- **Roles** — the billing roles available on the master, each with its own billable rate.
- **Work locations** — the master's locations (city, state, country), used by the Time grid's project → location cascade.
- **Invoices** — every invoice against this master (billing permissions only).

## People

People lists every member on the project, with an **Approvers** column (if you can see approver relationships) and an **Assistants** column (if you can see assistant relationships) — you always see your own row. **Edit approvers** opens a drawer to add or remove who approves a selected member's time; **My assistants** lets you name the members who can act as your assistant.

## Billing

### Accounts and Payment Details

**Accounts** lists your Stripe-backed billing accounts — name, balance, currency, invoice prefix, and contact details — with **New Account** to add one and drill-throughs to that account's invoices and, with billing permissions, its **Payment details**: bank transfer information (IBAN/SWIFT or account/routing, depending on currency) and any payment methods on file.

### Invoices

**Invoices** lists every invoice, filterable by status — **Open, Past Due, Draft, Paid, Voided, All** — and searchable by invoice number, customer, or master project. **New Invoice** starts a draft. Opening an invoice shows its header (customer, amounts, due date, status) over three tabs — **Items**, **Time**, **Trips**. **Add time** and **Add trips** swap the current tab in place for a picker of unbilled time or trips; select what belongs on this invoice and add it in bulk. A draft invoice can be edited or **Submit**ted, which assigns its invoice number and finalizes it; an open invoice can be **Void**ed. **Receipts PDF** downloads a single PDF merging every receipt behind the invoice's billed trips.

### Voids

**Voids** lists every invoice eligible to void — open or paid — so you can release its billed time and expenses back to the unbilled pool without hunting for the invoice first. Voiding is final: it can't be undone.

## What's Fixed Along the Way

This release also closes ownership gaps in the older screens the Project app replaces: reading another member's time, expenses, or receipts required being their approver, and a couple of screens didn't check that at all. Those are fixed, and no approver — on the new screens or the old ones — can approve their own timesheet or expense report, even from a self-approver relationship that already exists on the account.
