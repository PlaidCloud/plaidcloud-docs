## Subscribe

Looking ahead, every monthly summary lands here as it's published. Bookmark this page or follow our [product blog](https://plaidcloud.com/blog/) for announcements.

## How These Notes Are Produced

Each monthly summary aggregates the substantive PR-level changes that shipped to tenants during that calendar month. Pure infrastructure work (image bumps, internal refactors, RC cuts, secret rotation, observability tuning) is filtered out. If a month contained only maintenance work, it's listed in the "Earlier history" rollup rather than getting its own page.
