Every PlaidCloud workspace signs its own login sessions and AI-agent tokens with a secret that belongs only to that workspace. No signing secret is shared across workspaces.

## What Is Signed Per Workspace

- **Browser sessions** — the cookie that keeps you signed in to the web app.
- **AI-agent (MCP) tokens** — the credentials issued to AI agents that connect to your workspace's [MCP server](/integrations/ai-coding-agents/), plus the short-lived tokens workflow steps use to act on your behalf.

Each is signed with your workspace's own secret, so a session or token minted for one workspace is never valid in another.

## Why It Matters

Workspace isolation is a core PlaidCloud guarantee: your data, projects, and now your session and agent credentials are scoped to your workspace alone. A leaked or shared secret cannot be used to forge access into a different workspace.

The signing secrets are generated per workspace, held in a managed secrets vault, and never stored in source code or configuration files. They are not rotated on a schedule — only deliberately, such as during a security update.

## What You Need to Do

Nothing to configure — this is managed for you.

The first time your workspace applies its own secret (a one-time event), the previously shared secret is retired. That invalidates existing sessions and any tokens issued before the change:

- **Web app**: you are signed out once and sign back in normally.
- **AI agents**: each connected agent — Claude, Claude Desktop, Cursor, ChatGPT, and others — must re-authorize its connection to your workspace's MCP server.

<Aside type="tip">See [Signed Out or Disconnected After a Security Update](/integrations/ai-coding-agents/troubleshooting/#signed-out-or-disconnected-after-a-security-update) for how to reconnect each AI client.</Aside>

This happens once per workspace. Afterward, sessions and tokens behave exactly as before.
