**Usage Reports** shows who uses what in your workspace: which screens, dashboards, apps and notebooks people open, how many people come back, and where they go next. Use it to see what your members adopt, which dashboards, apps and notebooks earn their keep and which nobody uses, who hasn't been around, and where people drop out of a process.

Open it from the **Identity** tab, next to the Security Log.

## Who Can See It

Usage Reports needs the same permission as the Security Log: `identity.security_log.read`. Without it the button is not offered, and a member who opens the report anyway sees a notice that they don't have access.

The reports show usage per member in several places: the **Users** tab and its member profiles, the member list for each item on the **Content** tab, and the **Daily activity by member** and **First use** exports, which include member ids. Everything else is counted across members. Grant the permission only to people who should see individual activity.

Opening a named member's profile, listing the members who were **Not seen**, opening the member list for an item, and every CSV download each write an entry to the Security Log. See [Privacy and Audit](#privacy-and-audit).

## What Is Counted

Usage Reports combines these sources. Choose one from **Source**, or leave it on **All sources**.

| Source | What is recorded |
|---|---|
| PlaidCloud app | The screens members open and the actions they take in the app. |
| Superset | Dashboard and chart views. |
| Panel apps | App visits and the time members spend in each app. |
| App visits | Visits to Dash apps and WASM apps. |
| JupyterHub | Time members spend in JupyterHub sessions. |
| Jupyter notebooks | The notebooks members open and save. |

JupyterHub sessions and Jupyter notebooks are separate sources, so you can choose either one. Panel apps are a single source, and **App visits** covers Dash apps and WASM apps. Notebook events are recorded only for notebooks that run on the default notebook image. A custom image doesn't report them.

Events, sources and screens appear under readable names, such as **Opened Dash app**, **Opened WASM app**, **Opened notebook** and **App visits**, rather than as internal codes. A dashboard or app that has since been deleted shows as **Deleted dashboard** or **Deleted app**.

<Aside type="note">
Every day in the reports is a UTC day. A member working late in the evening in the Americas can appear on the next day.
</Aside>

## Read the Reports

Choose a source and a **Date range** at the top of the window, then select **Refresh**. The ranges are the last 7, 30, 90 or 180 days. Each tab loads when you first open it and again when you change the source or range.

### Overview

The headline numbers for the range:

| Number | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Active users** | Members with any activity. |
| **New users** | Members seen for the first time in the range. |
| **Returning users** | Active members who were first seen before the range. |
| **Activity minutes** | Estimated: five minutes for each five-minute period with any action or view. |
| **Events** | Everything recorded. |
| **Unattributed events** | Events no member could be named for. They count as events, not as users. |
| **App open minutes** | How long Panel apps and JupyterHub servers were open, idle time included. Counted apart from activity minutes. |

Each number shows its **Change** against the previous period of the same length, so a 30-day range is compared with the 30 days before it. **New** means the number went up from zero. A dash (**—**) means the previous period isn't fully covered by the history kept, so there is nothing fair to compare with.

When the history for your workspace starts inside the range, a **History starts** banner gives the date. Days before it show no activity because nothing was collected, not because nobody used the product.

**Adoption** lists each screen, dashboard, chart or item with its source, event, number of users, share of active users, events and unattributed events. **By month** shows active users, average daily users and stickiness for each month, with a note when the month is still running, only partly covered, or a frozen snapshot.

### Activity

Active users by **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly** period, plus **Retention by first week**: for each week members first appeared, how many came back in each week after. A period marked with an asterisk isn't all inside the range.

### Paths

The most taken moves between screens, as **Moved from**, **Moved to** and **Times taken**, across apps and sources. Paths cover the last 30 days at most.

### Users

Choose **Seen** or **Not seen**.

**Seen** has one row per member: name, username, active days, events, **Activity minutes**, **App open minutes**, and when they were first and last seen. Select a member to open their profile.

A member's profile shows their **Active days**, **Events**, **Activity minutes** and **App open minutes**, when they were first and last seen, a day-by-day chart of events, and the items they used, with each item's source, event, number of events and when it was last used. Items appear under their readable names, such as the dashboard or app title. Use **Back** to return to the list.

**Not seen** lists the members with no activity in the range, with their **Member since** and **Last seen** dates. **None recorded** means no activity in the history kept: at most 25 months, and only since collection began. Select **Download CSV** to save the list.

### Content

See how much each dashboard, Panel app, Dash app, WASM app and notebook is used. Filter by **Type**, or leave it on **All types**.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The item's name. A deleted item shows as **Deleted dashboard** or **Deleted app**. A notebook is counted once by its path, however many people's servers it is in. |
| **Type** | Dashboard, Panel app, Dash app, WASM app or Notebook. |
| **Users** | Distinct members who used it in the range. |
| **Adoption** | Users as a share of the active users in the range. |
| **Opens** | Dashboards: page loads. Panel apps: sessions started. Notebooks: opens, one per member per notebook per 30 minutes. Dash apps and WASM apps: visits. |
| **App open minutes** | Panel apps only. Time the app was open, idle time included. |
| **Trend** | Opens per week in the range, oldest first. |
| **Last used (UTC)** | When a member last used it. |

Items nobody used in the range are listed too, so you can see what to retire. Select **Hide unused** to leave them out. Select an item to see the members who used it, with their **Opens** and when each last used it. **Download CSV** saves either the item list or an item's members.

Notebooks appear once someone has opened them. Dash app and WASM app visits are counted only from when this release reached your workspace, and a note above the list says when collection began for each type.

If the names of dashboards and apps can't be looked up, a warning says **Names are unavailable right now, so items nobody used are not listed**. The items that were used still appear.

### Exports

Download a summary as a CSV. Choose the **Summary** and a **Date range** of up to 25 months, then select **Download CSV**.

| Summary | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Daily activity** | Users and events for each day, source, event and subject. |
| **Daily activity by member** | The same for each member, with member ids. |
| **Monthly adoption** | Users and events for each month, source, event and subject. |
| **Monthly active users** | Active users for each month and source. |
| **First use** | When each member first used each source, with member ids. |

The export uses the source you chose above the tabs. A large summary downloads in batches of up to 50,000 rows, and the window combines them into one file. If the file would be larger than the window builds, it saves what it has and tells you the file is incomplete: choose a shorter range to get the rest.

Every export writes an entry to the Security Log recording who exported which summary, the range, and the number of rows. A summary that spans several batches writes an entry for each batch.

<Aside type="caution">
The monthly summaries include a total row for each event, with the subject `*`. That row is the distinct users and events across all of the event's subjects. Don't add it to the subject rows or you will count everything twice.
</Aside>

### Funnel

See how many members go through a sequence of steps. A step is an event from one of the sources, for a particular subject, or **Any subject**.

1. Select **Add step** and choose the source, event, and subject for each step. A funnel has 2 to 6 steps.
2. Choose **Time allowed between steps**: 1, 6, 24, 72, or 168 hours.
3. Choose a **Date range** of 7, 30, or 90 days.
4. Select **Run funnel**.

The result shows, for each step, how many members reached it, the share of those who started, how many stopped there, and the median time from the previous step. Steps can mix sources, so you can follow a member from a PlaidCloud screen to a Superset dashboard. **Recent funnels** reopens a funnel you ran before. A step list only offers events recorded in the range.

## Unattributed Usage

Some events can't be tied to a PlaidCloud member. They count in **Events** and in **Unattributed events**, but never in user counts. The main cases:

- A Superset account created inside Superset instead of through PlaidCloud.
- A view of an embedded dashboard, which has no Superset user.
- Panel and JupyterHub activity the platform logs can't tie to a member. Their attribution comes from those logs, so treat it as best-effort.

A large unattributed share on a source means its user counts are understated.

## When History Starts

History begins when usage collection is turned on for your workspace, and a day or month before then shows no activity. The first month with data is marked as partial.

Panel and JupyterHub are read from platform logs, which are kept for about 90 days. When collection starts, those two sources fill in at most that much earlier history. Superset fills in from its own logs, up to 180 days. Only the PlaidCloud app starts from nothing when collection starts.

Usage Reports keeps detailed activity for 180 days, which is why the longest range is 180 days, and keeps first and last use for each member and item for 25 months, along with monthly summaries. A range that starts earlier than 180 days ago shows a warning instead of the **Not seen** list and the items nobody used, because the detail needed to tell who was absent is gone. When a member is removed outside PlaidCloud, for example through SCIM or Keycloak, their raw usage ages out after 180 days and their first-use record after 25 months.

## Privacy and Audit

Viewing usage for named members is itself audited. These actions write an entry to the Security Log recording who did it:

- Opening a named member's profile.
- Listing the members who were **Not seen**.
- Opening the list of members who used an item.
- Every CSV download, from the **Exports** tab, the **Not seen** list or the **Content** tab.

Only members with the `identity.security_log.read` permission can open the report at all.

## Erase a Member's Usage

A workspace administrator (or super user) with the `identity.security_log.read` permission can erase one member's usage and stop collecting it, then lift the erasure later. Both erase and restore require that permission and that role. These two actions are available through the REST API only.

| Action | Request |
|---|---|
| Erase | `DELETE /rest/v1/identity/usage/users/{user_id}` |
| Restore | `DELETE /rest/v1/identity/usage/users/{user_id}/erasure` |

Erasing removes the member's events and first-use record, and from that moment the reports and exports leave the member out. Their usage isn't collected again, and history that is read in later from Panel and JupyterHub logs is dropped too. If the removal can't finish at once, the response says so and the next hourly maintenance run completes it.

Restoring lifts the erasure so new activity is collected again. It doesn't bring back what was erased.

Both actions write an entry to the Security Log.
